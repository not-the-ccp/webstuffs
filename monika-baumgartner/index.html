Lot 314

Monika Baumgartner — Personal Archive and Working Library

German television actress and theatre director (born 19 July 1951)

Monika Baumgartner was born on 19 July 1951 and trained in Munich before joining the Bayerisches Staatsschauspiel, where she directed productions of the folk-play repertoire and first became known for her uncanny ear for Bavarian speech. On television she built a forty-year career playing the sturdy, warm-hearted, occasionally formidable women of Bavaria — innkeepers, widows, landladies, and the sort of character who settles every village dispute over a kitchen table with a pot of coffee and a slice of cake. Her most enduring role was the widowed landlady of the Gasthof zum Bären in the long-running Heimat series filmed near Rosenheim, a part she played from 1992 until 2003, in which she wore the same black apron and silver brooch in every episode.

The lot comprises her working library and personal archive: two annotated prompt copies of Der Brandner Kaspar (1983 and the 1991 revival), with blocking notes in pencil and cast contact details on the flyleaves; a theatre director's notebook for the 1987 Landestheater Schwaben production of Die Jagd nach dem Glück; sixteen original television scripts from the Rosenheim series, 1992–2003, carrying her marginalia, costume annotations, and tea-stained page corners; a set of wardrobe fabric swatches labelled by character; and a leather-bound album of press cuttings, 1976–2014, tracing her progress from provincial theatre to national television.

The desk copy of Malayang Kilusan ng Mamamayang Zambaleño — Liner Notes .

Also included: a silver brooch in the form of an edelweiss, worn on screen in seventeen episodes; a pair of spectacles with tortoiseshell arms, used for reading scripts in the dressing room; and an address book with entries in three alphabets, including the Munich stage-door keeper, the Rosenheim postmistress, and a Manila printer whose name appears only once, beside a telephone number that no longer connects.

Estimate

£8,000 – 12,000

Provenance

From the estate of Monika Baumgartner, by descent. Consigned by her nephew, Maximilian Baumgartner, of Munich.

Condition

Generally good. The scripts show the usual wear of working copies — coffee stains, pencil marks, and dog-eared pages consistent with rehearsal use. The album binding is intact; the brooch has minor surface wear; the spectacles are complete with both lenses.

Literature

Baumgartner, M. “Auf der Bühne und vor der Kamera.” Theater heute, March 1987, pp. 44–49.
