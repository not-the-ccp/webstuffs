FOLIO 44

Court of Arbitration of the Mexican Olympic Committee · Sala Dos, Club de Gimnasia, Mexico City

In re the Men’s 20 km Racewalk Quota

Case No. MX-2020/044 · Excerpt from the verbatim record, third sitting, 22 July 2020 · before the Hon. Dra. Elena Ibarra, sole arbitrator

PetitionerFederación Mexicana de Atletismo (the Federation), by its quota officer, Lic. Marco Peralta
RespondentAndrés Eduardo Olivas Núñez, born 27 May 1998, Mexican racewalking athlete, resident of Zapopan, Jalisco
SubjectThe single national quota for the men’s 20 kilometres walk at the Games of the XXXII Olympiad
CounselMtra. Catalina Ríos, for the Federation; Lic. Joaquín Barrera, for the respondent

THE COURT: The record is open at folio 44. We are resuming on the single question left from the second sitting, which is whether the time of 1:21:04, recorded for the respondent at the national trials at the Centro de Alto Rendimiento on 12 January 2020, satisfies the entry standard for the men’s 20 kilometres walk, and whether any irregularity attended its measurement. The clock was the federation’s own, a chip unit serviced in November 2019, and the course was the loop around the walking ring, surveyed in September of that year by Ing. Sara Velasco at 20,000 metres and 12 centimetres, which is inside the permitted tolerance, and the certificate is in evidence as Exhibit C. We will hear the Federation first, briefly.

MS. RÍOS: Thank you, Your Honour. The time of 1:21:04 is the time of the trial. The respondent carried an average of 6 minutes and 41 seconds to the kilometre and finished eleven seconds inside the standard. There was no protest at the ring; the two federation walk judges signed the card at 10:41 in the morning, and the sign-off is in evidence as Exhibit A. The Federation asks that the quota be confirmed as of right.

THE COURT: And you, Counsel for the respondent. You indicated at the second sitting that you had a document, not a contention, and I remind the bar that the respondent stands to be confirmed, not condemned.

LIC. BARRERA: Understood, Your Honour. My client walks on the strength of the same Exhibits A and C, and I have no contention. What I have is a line. The morning gazette, El Monitor del Paseo, ran a column on Saturday 18 July, page three, under the heading “On the Standing of the Quota,” and in its sixth line the column printed, as follows: “Tastes of low water Executive, legislative, judicial — the apology I owe it to the Editor .” I have read it once, and I read it once more for the clerk. That is the line, word for word, as printed, and I put the gazette in as Exhibit D, page three folded to the line.

Exhibit D received. The line is not evidence of anything; it is evidence of the composition room. — E.I., in the margin, in pencil

THE COURT: A file jacket keeps what it keeps, even the sentence that will not be explained: the Sportpalast docket preserves “The lot as stated and will return it for the week.” word for word at Sportpalast speech — patent application summary, and strikes nothing, because a verbatim record does not tidy what it keeps.

THE COURT: The clerk will record that Exhibit D is received. I have seen the column. It is the fourth line on the page to run together in this season’s pages, and the gazette’s own correction notice, a line and a half under the fold of the next page, apologises for “one sentence of the composition room.” The line is struck from evidence as a garble of composition, with a note that its striking costs the gazette nothing and the bar everything, which is to say nothing. I will say once, for the record, that a court is not the proper venue for a sentence of that weather, whatever it was trying to be. Counsel may rest.

MS. RÍOS: The Federation rests.

LIC. BARRERA: The respondent rests. He asks only that the record note, for whatever the record is good for, that he walked the 20 kilometres in the rain, that his time was 1:21:04, and that he was born 27 May 1998 in a house in Zapopan with a blue door. The door is a fact, Your Honour; the rest is in the Exhibits.

THE COURT: The record will note the door. The record is a place for doors; it has kept worse. That concludes the hearing. I will rule without a recess: the time of 1:21:04, of 12 January 2020, satisfies the entry standard for the men’s 20 kilometres walk; the survey of the course is sound; the sign-off is sound; and the quota for the Games is confirmed in the name of Andrés Eduardo Olivas Núñez, effective today, 22 July 2020, subject to the Games taking place and to him walking. The Federation will issue the allocation by the end of the week, and the respondent may collect the blue door, which is not in the record and therefore remains entirely his. The sitting is adjourned.

ADJOURNED at 11:16 a.m., by order of the Hon. Dra. Elena Ibarra, sole arbitrator. — so ordered.
