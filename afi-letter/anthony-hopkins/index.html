AFI Archive Broadcast Service · Program Note

The Dragon’s Rehearsal

A radio play in two scenes and a coda, broadcast 26 December 2008 · 23:05 · 4.555 MHz, midnight window

The play below was written for the AFI Archive Broadcast Service and recorded on the night of 26 December 2008, from the vault room at 2021 North Western Avenue in Los Angeles, at four degrees. It was made after the telephone call: twenty minutes before airtime, the announcement came up through the public address that the BAFTA Fellowship for lifetime achievement had been given to Anthony Hopkins, and the studio decided to broadcast a play about him instead of the scheduled reel survey. The man who wrote the play, Silas Wren, had written about this building once before, in 1979, from a booth in the Arcadia, and his old jokes are kept: the chicory coffee, the cracked saucer, the NCR cash drawer that chimes when it is liable to become soft, and the 1914 Keystone short in which Charlie Chaplin — set down, to the foot of the nitrate, in the Charlie Chaplin — Encyclopaedia Entry — reduced a mail-order bookcase to kindling with a janitor’s hatchet. The play moves between that vault, a rehearsal room on Gower Street in 1958, and one telephone line in Port Talbot.

Cast. ANTHONY (a young Welshman, 20); LAURENCE (slender, 35, a hat in his lap); SILAS WREN (producer and archivist); MAGGIE FENN (nitrate conservator). Music and sound effects. The vault dial; the NCR drawer; the Gower Street radiator; one bell.

Scene One — The Vault Room, AFI, 2021 North Western Avenue, Los Angeles · 22:40

[The air handler. A temperature dial ticking. Far off, from the Arcadia next door, the NCR cash drawer chimes once, softly, and then does not chime again.]

MAGGIE

It’s holding at four degrees, Silas. The nitrate sleeps the whole way down.

SILAS

The whole way down. Ninety-five feet of Mabel’s Married Life in tank seven. Last full survey of this room is dated 1968.

MAGGIE

(reading, flat) “Fire inspection, 3 March 1968. All tanks sealed. In the absence of fires throughout the 1940s. Recommendation: none.”

SILAS

That was the theory, then. That a film kept cold would keep forever, the way a book does. And the catalogue keeps the count the way a census does. Tonight we are not here for the film, though. We are here for the call.

MAGGIE

The Welshman.

SILAS

Born Port Talbot, 31 December 1937. Trained at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama, graduated 1957, then up to London to RADA, where — as the play has it — he is spotted in the back row by a man with a hat. In 1993 the Queen knighthood came, for services to drama. And two Academy Awards, four BAFTAs, two Primetime Emmy Awards, and a Laurence Olivier Award sit in a cabinet at home that, I am told, holds nothing else of consequence.

MAGGIE

What does it hold?

SILAS

One moustache, a cane, and a very good bookcase. Backwards.

[The NCR drawer chimes, across the street, three times.]

Scene Two — Rehearsal Room, RADA, Gower Street, London · February 1958

[A radiator. Felt footsteps on a wooden floor. Pages of a script turning, one at a time.]

ANTHONY

(20, Welsh) The boy is not afraid of the dog. He is afraid of the room.

VOICE

(back of the room) That’s right.

ANTHONY

I beg your —? There is no one in this room.

LAURENCE

The room. You’re playing the room, not the dog. (A chair.) My name’s Laurence. I came to see who is playing Lear in the autumn, and I’ll say this, which I don’t usually do: when you read that line I forgot I had come at all.

ANTHONY

I mean — you are —.

LAURENCE

I am thirty-five and I am tired of actors who have been to many places. Tell me where you have been.

ANTHONY

Port Talbot. The Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama, 1957. This building.

LAURENCE

Port Talbot. Keep that. It is the one thing in the syllabus no one can teach, and it is in your voice like a tide. Come in the autumn. Read for Lear, and read for nothing else.

[The radiator. The felt footsteps, going. The pages stop turning.]

Coda — The Studio, 2021 North Western Avenue · 23:05

[The studio clock. A telephone, ringing once, and answered.]

SILAS

(into the microphone) This is the AFI Archive Broadcast Service, recording from the vault room in Los Angeles, 23:05. The announcement came up through the PA twenty minutes ago: the BAFTA Fellowship, for lifetime achievement, to Anthony Hopkins. With the Cecil B. DeMille Award of 2005, given on our own stage, the honours are now complete on paper, which is to say in the catalogue, which is to say in the census.

ANTHONY

(on the telephone, Port Talbot) You are broadcasting this at eleven-fifteen at night?

SILAS

The vault is quietest at eleven.

ANTHONY

And the dragon?

SILAS

Rehearsed. Every night since 1958.

[One bell. Then the vault dial, ticking. The transmission ends on the dial.]

Recorded live to disc in the vault room, tank seven sealed, room at four degrees. Transcribed from the broadcast disc for the service’s papers. The reel of Mabel’s Married Life was not struck; it slept through.

American Film Institute — A Letter to the Editor — the 1979 letter from the Arcadia that this play inherits.
