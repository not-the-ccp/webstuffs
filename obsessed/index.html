Deaths · Universal City, California

Obsessed

stylized in all lowercase · single · Geffen Records

22 March 2024 – 14 November 2025

FILED21 · 11 · 25 · cabinet 9

It is with a quiet cutter and a heavy stylus that we announce the death of Obsessed — styled in all lowercase — who died on the morning of Friday, 14 November 2025, at the Geffen cutting room in Universal City, California. She was one year, seven months and twenty-three days old. Born in Los Angeles on 22 March 2024, she was released as a single alongside Guts (Spilled), the deluxe edition of Rodrigo's second studio album, Guts (2023), and spent her public life on the radio, the turntable and the streaming services of the world.

She was written by Olivia Rodrigo with the American musician St. Vincent — Annie Clark — and her producer, Dan Nigro, at a room on Sunset Boulevard with the windows shut against the Santa Ana wind. A pop-punk, power pop, dance-rock and pop rock song, carrying grunge, alternative rock, pop metal and punk rock influences, she detailed Rodrigo's jealousy and preoccupation with her partner's ex-girlfriend. She arrived at 154 beats per minute: distorted guitars in the first eight seconds, a chorus before the listener was ready for it, and a bridge that named nobody and implied everybody.

Music critics believed she achieved an edgier sound than Rodrigo's previous output and directed praise towards her upbeat nature and her willingness to make jealousy sound like a party. She debuted at number 39 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the chart week of 6 April 2024, reached number 7 on the UK Singles Chart, and passed 410 million streams on Spotify before her masters were withdrawn in October 2025. A seven-inch followed in June, pressed on black vinyl at a plant in Nashville, with a live recording from the Fonda Theatre on the B-side.

The end came quietly after the final pressing run of three thousand copies. A week later I took an offcut of lignum vitae — dense enough to argue with. It went under the headblock of the cutting lathe as a damper, and I cut one last reference lacquer at forty-five revolutions per minute, running the groove inward until the stylus ran out of wax. The disc now sits in a manila envelope at cabinet 9, labelled in pencil: no further cuts.

Survived by: her writer Olivia Rodrigo; her co-writers Annie Clark and Dan Nigro; Geffen Records; and the songs of Guts (Spilled), who miss her at track seven. She is also survived by one gold test pressing, unnumbered, and a mis-pressed label that reads “OBSESSED” in caps, against all instruction.

A private pressing was cut on 18 November 2025. The lacquer was interred in the tape vault at Geffen, Universal City, on 21 November 2025, at a service attended by six engineers, one microphone and no speakers. In lieu of flowers, the family asks that she be played loud, once, with the windows open.

Other notices in this ledger: Hannu Rajaniemi (1978–2047), kept at Clarke's desk in Edinburgh; Daylight Saving Time — Sana Valley Line, Summer Service 2024–25, which kept the hour she never did; CD&V — Field Notes, Flanders, where a green rosette has not faded; and Regiões do Brasil, five regions remembered at the edge of a delta.
