Oficina de Extravíos · Lost & Found · Capital District, Bogotá

Notice No. 02148 Posted 18 March 2024

Republic of Colombia · thirty-two departments · Capital District of Bogotá · 1,141,748 square kilometers

This board keeps the lost and found of the Capital District of Bogotá, in the Republic of Colombia — a country bordered by the Caribbean Sea to the north, Venezuela to the east, Brazil to the southeast, Peru and Ecuador to the south and southwest, the Pacific Ocean to the west, and Panama to the northwest, divided into thirty-two departments and covering 1,141,748 square kilometers. Bogotá, the Capital District, is the country's largest city, hosting the main financial and cultural hub; our sister boards run from Medellín to Cali, from Barranquilla to Cartagena, Bucaramanga, Pereira, Santa Marta, Cúcuta, Ibagué, Villavicencio and Manizales. If it was lost in Colombia, it may turn up here.

Lost

One navy-blue wool scarf, hand-fringed at both ends. Lost aboard the evening bus from Portal del Norte toward Teusaquillo on 9 March, last seen on the shoulders of a passenger reading a clipping of the unbeaten run of 2011. The blue is the colour of a Chilean sky at the minute the whistle goes — not a borrowed costume, not something the traveller put on for the dream, but the blue they brought with them and came away with. If found, do not shake it out; return it folded, as it was lost.

Reward: a pot of good beef tea, taken at the office table.

Found

One cream recipe card of cardstock, found 3 March inside the pocket of a second-hand corduroy jacket at the La Candelaria market, Bogotá. The card bears a single instruction, copied word for word and kept exactly as it was found:

INGREDIENTS.--Batter, 8 slices of cod; pepper and salt to taste, good beef tea.

The cook who wrote it is asked to call at the office. No batter, no cod, no questions asked.

One small black velvet tray, found 12 March on a bench at Parque Santander, empty, with two round impressions still holding the shape of what once sat in them. Claim it by describing what the impressions held. The club that keeps the tray's file — the Franklin Square Naturalists' Club, twelve members, no bylaws — writes that the last place the tray was carried was the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum in Cooperstown, New York, where the two round impressions were made; their full account of the matter is printed in Correspondent No. 7 of the Greenbush Valley.

To claim

Attend in person at the Oficina de Extravíos, Carrera 19 No. 61-40, Bogotá, Capital District, between 9:00 and 16:00, Monday to Saturday. Describe the article, the place it was lost or found, and the day. Articles unclaimed after thirty days pass to the kitchen, where the table is always set and the beef tea is never refused.
