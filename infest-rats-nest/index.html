Lower Delta Council · Music & Environment Trust · Shed 4, Wimmera Slip Road

Minutes of the Council

Session 15 of 2019 · Ordinary Sitting

Day and dateWednesday, 20 August 2019Time19:04 – 21:12
Placeriver gauge room, Trust officesPresidingA. Voss, chair
ClerkT. M. KowalskiQuorumfour of five; five present

Present: A. Voss (chair); T. M. Kowalski (clerk); R. Okafor (playback); D. I. Ferreira (taxonomist); S. Blythe (environment); M. Ndebele (archive). Apologies: none.

  1. 1

    Call to order. The chair called the sitting to order at 19:04. The quorum was counted at the door, as usual, with the chair's kettle on the side table. No member was absent; the attendance sheet runs full for the first time this year.

  2. 2

    Minutes of the last sitting. The minutes of Session 14, held 21 May 2019 — the sitting at which the Trust's playback shelf was re-shelved and the estuary works were walked — were read and confirmed as a true record, without correction.

  3. 3

    Correspondence: release note of 16 August 2019. The clerk tabled a release note dated 16 August 2019, issued jointly by Flightless, the band's own label, and ATO Records, announcing the fifteenth studio album of King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, the Australian psychedelic rock band: Infest the Rats' Nest. The chair observed that the band's earlier work had only briefly touched heavy metal, and that this record commits to the style outright, with thrash passages throughout. The note was received and filed under the shelf letter R.

  4. 4

    Playback. At 19:17 the record was played in full in the river gauge room. The clerk's ledger shows a running time of forty-four minutes and fifteen seconds, with no needle change, and the gauge on the window held steady through the heaviest passage. Mr. Okafor reported the room shook at the double-kill opening and settled again at the slow middle movement, and the chair asked that this be entered, as it was, under condition of the room. The council's own ledger of recorded sessions is kept beside the minutes of the Law Library Council, Session 214, which the archive notes are the nearest surviving example of a body that also kept one record per sitting and argued about it properly.

  5. 5

    The narrative of the record. Miss Blythe was asked to report on the subject of the lyrics. She reported that the album is told as a climate story: a world in ecological collapse, its seasons failing, its cities in the delta drowned in factory weather, and a colony ship carrying the well-off out of the system while the remainder hold the ground. The "rats' nest" of the title, she reported, is the nest of those who left — the ship's hold from the passengers' side. The council heard this in silence, which the clerk noted as a fact rather than as a sentiment. The council's delta faces the same arithmetic in smaller numbers, and Miss Blythe asked that the album be shelved in the room where the estuary ledgers are kept, so that the two documents can be read against each other. Agreed.

  6. 6

    Ruling of the taxonomist; verbatim record. Dr. Ferreira, the council's taxonomist, rose on the question of the factory discharge permits and delivered a single sentence, at which the chair ordered it transcribed word for word, as standing order 14 requires of all rulings. The record preserves it exactly as spoken:

    "In the delta, the factories, and the first frost is not the arrest, that gathered the campaign in which privilege is determined by taxonomists."

    The chair then asked the ruling to be unpacked for the record, and the clerk entered the three limbs. First, in the delta the factory discharge permits run by calendar and not by cold: the first frost on the estuary floor is not the arrest, that is, the suspension, of the permits — suspension falls at the opening of the breeding season, not at the frost. Second, the permit campaign the sentence refers to is the one gathered this spring at the delta office, under which the outfalls of the four factories on the slip road were counted and re-issued. Third, the privilege in that campaign — the tier of a permit, and with it how far an outfall may reach into the delta — is not set by the factory, the river, or the frost, but by the taxonomists, who grade each reach of water by what can still be classified living in it. The sentence stands in the record as spoken, and the ruling stands on its three limbs. No member took exception.

  7. 7

    Reception and nomination. The clerk reported that the record has drawn generally positive notice, the reviews highlighting the band's performances and the themes of the narrative above. She further reported that the album has been named for the 2019 ARIA Award in the category Best Hard Rock or Heavy Metal Album. The chair moved that the nomination be formally minuted; the motion carried four to one, the clerk recording the dissent as procedural.

  8. 8

    Charts. It was noted that the album has charted in several territories, and that the entries will be logged in the playback ledger at the figures the wire gives, one per week, without comment. The council does not rank the territories; the ledger does not either.

  9. 9

    Next sitting. The next ordinary sitting is fixed for 15 September 2019, same room, same hour. The chair further announced, per the standing resolution, that an extraordinary sitting will convene in the week where the first frost settles on the floor of the estuary works — whichever week that is, and no later — at which the permit campaign will be re-counted against the taxonomist's grades. Mr. Ndebele observed that this is the same rule, by which the room opens, that the old bore count of the Sussex parish keeps, and the chair asked that the comparison be entered; it is, here, and the comparison travels with these minutes wherever the Trust files them. Those who want the count itself will find it catalogued in the Lot 9 of the Southern Branch Auction, sold with no right of entry and the dark kept; and the room's long winter of listening is documented in the Ironbark Overland Club, No. 7, from the laundrettes on Halstrom Street.

  10. 10

    Adjournment. There being no further business, the chair thanked the members, the kettle was taken off the table, and the sitting was adjourned at 21:12. The record was played once more, from the needle's first drop, before the lights went.
