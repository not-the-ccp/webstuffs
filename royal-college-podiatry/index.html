The Craft

The Royal College of Podiatry (RCPod) is the professional association and trade union for registered chiropodists and podiatrists in the United Kingdom. Each year, RCPod releases its flagship instrument, designed to meet the exacting standards of clinical practice. The Professional Care Instrument is forged in the College's Birmingham workshop by master artisans who have trained in the traditional methods of foot care instrument making.

The process begins with selecting high-carbon stainless steel from certified European suppliers. The steel is tempered to 58-62 Rockwell hardness, ensuring the edge holds through thousands of clinical applications. The handle is crafted from sustainably harvested walnut, hand-turned on a lathe and polished to a smooth finish that fits naturally in the hand.

The instrument ships complete with a custom leather case, maintenance instructions, and a certificate of registration from RCPod. Each unit is individually numbered and tracked through the College's quality assurance system. The Professional Care Instrument is available for purchase by active members of RCPod through the annual subscription program, or as a one-time acquisition for institutional customers.

Technical Specifications

  • Overall Length: 8.5 inches (216 mm)
  • Blade Length: 3.2 inches (81 mm)
  • Weight: 45 grams (1.6 oz)
  • Material: High-carbon stainless steel, walnut handle
  • Finish: Matte black with brass accents
  • Registration: Individual RCPod serial number
  • Warranty: 5 years against manufacturing defects

Clinical Applications

The Professional Care Instrument is designed for use in podiatric practices across the UK. It excels in:

  • Calus removal and debridement
  • Nail avulsion and trimming
  • Corn and callus reduction
  • Minor surgical procedures
  • Routine foot maintenance
