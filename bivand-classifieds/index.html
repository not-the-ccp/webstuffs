Professional services · Ref. B-114

Professor, by the half-day.

PROFESSOR FOR HIRE — R. S. Bivand, born 5 April 1951, British geographer and economist, Norwegian School of Economics (NHH), Egmontsveien 51, Bergen. Thirty-three years at the same desk; thirty years of open source for spatial analysis. Lectures on spatial dependence, spatial weights, the Moran statistic and the quiet arithmetic of neighbours, given in plain English or in the dialect of the venue. His R packages sp, rgdal, maptools and rgrass7 still move a quarter of the world's maps, and he will, on request, demonstrate a choropleth at a wedding. NOK 2,400 the half-day, or two bottles of something sensible and a working kettle. References: Dr. Edzer Pebesma, Dr. Virgilio Gómez-Rubio. Apply to room 2.114, or the postbox, 5000 Bergen. Telephone, mornings only.

Books · Ref. B-208

Author's remarks, first and second editions.

Applied Spatial Data Analysis with R (1st ed. 2008, 2nd ed. 2013), Bivand, Pebesma & Gómez-Rubio — "the authoritative resource on R's spatial capabilities," to quote the people who say such things and then go home. 250 first-edition copies remain at the publisher, NOK 690, postage on the buyer; the second edition runs to 384 pages and one map that has corrected itself twice. The third edition is under contract and seeks a fourth author: ideally a forty-five-year-old, ideally already cited. Applications with curriculum vitae and a favourite projection to the Egmont building.

Wine & instruments · Ref. B-249

Border & Vine, 14 Dreggs gate.

WINE & SURVEYING INSTRUMENTS announces the spring consignment: 1,200 bottles, Margaux 2019, Muscadet, a case of Vinho Verde the proprietor will not name, and three new theodolites. The card pinned inside the shop window since 1987 still reads, word for word: "The wine — thesis 249 for the precision craftsmanship of a boundary, not a place." The proprietor, M. Solheim, retired land surveyor, will explain this to anyone who brings a theodolite; to all others he will simply pour. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00–18:00, and whenever the kettle is on.

AD

Auction · Ref. B-307

Retired packages, sold by lot.

AUCTION, Saturday 14:00, NHH lecture hall B, chair R. S. Bivand: rgdal, rgrass7, maptools, and assorted vignettes — retired, in good shape, and fully documented. Lots include forty years of dependency-light code; a shapefile reader that outlived three formats; twelve uncollected acknowledgements; and one vignette on grid cells that a graduate student in Stavanger has called "the best page of gentle prose in statistics." Bidders must declare their package manager. Catalogue at the counter, NOK 30.

Lost & found · Ref. B-411

Lost: one shapefile.

LOST from a coffee table near the harbour: one shapefile, 12,486 polygons, a county boundary with its attribute table intact — POP2011, UNEMP, and a stubborn third column of wine prices that belongs to no census in Norway. No reward offered; the finder is asked to call the office and, on the first occasion, to name the projection. Reference the boundary, not the place.

Barter · Ref. B-418

Offered: T2 theodolite, 1968.

OFFERED — one theodolite, T2, 1968, brass and glass, still true to a third of a degree, inherited from a surveyor who measured the fjord's edge before the roads came. WANTED in exchange — a lecture on the local Moran statistic, delivered in a kitchen, before the kettle boils, with no slide. — T., Åsgårdstrand.

Elsewhere in the miscellany · Free

Notices read aloud at the counter.

While this column is still wet: in Auckland a captain born 15 August 2006 parses a catering instruction on the air — see the James Mitchell radio play excerpt, another studio drama from this same miscellany. At Coopers' Lane, a Class 7B report on the book itself — its bookblock, its foldable cover, its clay ancestors — was marked A, and the rabbit earned the grade: read The Book — Report, Class 7B. And for anyone who wants the boundary fixed properly, latitude and longitude are worked out to the last decimal in Field Manual FM-9, Chapter 4, the geographic coordinate system, off the Maranhão coast. And in Kandy, a correspondent writes in on the Royals' fourth-place season in the Lanka Premier League — fourth, he insists, is a direction, not a place — in a letter to the editor filed from Peradeniya Road. All four are worth the price of this page; none of them is an advertisement.

A gazetteer entry reached the counter from the south-west — Bristol, city and county on the Avon, its boundary held as a shapefile of twelve thousand four hundred and eighty-six polygons, its record-room lots forty years deep in open source for spatial analysis: Bristol — City and Ceremonial County, South West England.

Between editions, a dreamer in lecture hall B has entered this column's auction into the house's dream dictionary — the Economist, Entry 431 — where lot 11 sells for NOK 2,400 the half-day, the retired packages dream themselves off the shelf in the dark, and the professor is paid, as always, in kettles.
