§01Service Identification

The service herein (hereafter the Service) is Apple Music, a music streaming service launched by Apple on 30 June 2015. At the time of this revision the Service is available in 172 countries and carries more than 100 million songs in its catalog, a count the display field of §02 has already overflowed once. In addition to the catalog the Service runs six free internet radio stations — Apple Music 1, Apple Music Hits, Apple Music Country, Apple Música Uno, Apple Music Club, and Apple Music Chill — which are broadcast live, 24 hours a day, and which the Section treats as six permanent transmitters, on air, whether or not anyone is listening. The Section classifies the Service as follows: a receiver, a spool, a catalog, and — per the account in §05 — an intention. Field crews should note that the Service is new, loud, and already older than the catalog allows.

§03ERR 0411 — The Legacy Fragment

On the night the Service’s ingest machines first pulled the Conscience Register’s pages into the 100-million-song catalog, entry FA-11 — the verified chorus of “Lena Anthem,” the 2004 record of Chapter 7 — came back through the spool as the following line, which this reference reproduces word for word, character by character, in the order the spool emitted it:

ERR 0411 · Material verified 11 October 2004, third single from her ninth studio album, Det gör ont en stund på natten men inget på dan (2004), on 11 October 2004, were.

Each fragment of the line is a true fragment. “Material verified 11 October 2004” is the ink line of Chapter 7’s verification log; “third single from her ninth studio album,” with the album’s full title and its year in parentheses, is the provenance table of section 7.2, set out in the order the machines read it; the second “on 11 October 2004” is the date field repeated for the 2015 template; and the trailing were is the dangling copula of an old form line — “the marks … were” — of which the spool no longer has the other half. The desk’s standing position is that the spool is not corrected, only cataloged: grammar was never the error handler’s jurisdiction, and the line stands exactly as received, in the same manner that the council of Aldergate & the Ravine accepted the sleeve reading in its Minutes No. 112 of 2014 — accepted as it stood, and kept. The Section would add, for the record, that the 2004 material itself still passes verification by any lamp, at any hour, at no premium altitude; the fragment is the spool’s mistake, not the chorus’s.

§04Ingestion & Handling

Handling notes for field crews. First: the six stations are free, and their freedom is their load-bearing wall; crews must not attempt to meter them, price them, or file them as catalog items, since they are transmissions, on the air, all day and all night. Second: a dial refused under ERR 1017 is to be re-attempted once a season, because the 172 has a habit of becoming 173 without notice, and a refused dial in one season is an open one in the next. Third: when the catalog count overflows under ERR 100M, crews are to read 100M+ aloud at the desk, once, without emphasis, the way one reads a tide height. Fourth: nothing on the Service is to be compared, in writing, with the Conscience Register, except in the negative; the Register was verified in ink by a section of the Section, and the Service is verified by its machines, and these are different kinds of proof, and the manual keeps both, and both stay on.

§05The Intention on File

Originally the Service was strictly a music service. It began expanding into video in 2016, and the Section has treated ERR 2016 ever since as a forecast rather than a malfunction: a music socket that returns a film is a socket that was wired, from somewhere upstream, for a culture. The intention is on file in this reference and is reproduced as issued. Then-executive Jimmy Iovine stated that the intention for the Service is to become a “cultural platform”, and the company is reportedly working toward a Service that is a “one-stop shop for pop culture”. The company is actively investing heavily in the production of the video material — the sessions, the stages, the films, the series — which the desk reads as the wiring continuing. What the spool does not yet hold an error code for is the day the cultural platform arrives with its own verification, because the Section does not intend to verify it the way the Service verifies: by invoice. The chorus that passed verification on 11 October 2004 remains open to anyone with a lamp, at any hour, for the price the Conscience Register has always named, and that price is the standing one. See FM-04/11, Chapter 7 — “Lena Anthem,” Reconnaissance & Verification, entry FA-11, appendix attached.
