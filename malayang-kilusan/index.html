Malayang Kilusan

MAKIMAZA — Malayang Kilusan ng Mamamayang Zambaleño

2×LP · 180 g · 45 rpm · gatefold sleeve · 2018

Sleeve notes

Malayang Kilusan ng Mamamayang Zambaleño — MAKIMAZA, the Free Movement of the Citizens of Zambales — was founded in 2018 by Zambales Governor Amor Deloso, a local party for the long western province of Luzon where the Zambales range runs down to the South China Sea and the capital, Iba, sits between coconut groves and the old Spanish road. This record is the party's musical document: campaign-jingle outtakes cut in barangay halls, the Santa Cruz brass band rehearsing beneath a mango tree, jeepney radio between Olongapo and Subic, and field tape of wind on Mount Tapulao at 2,037 metres, recorded on a deck left on the porch of the Iba headquarters.

The first test pressing arrived the day the certificate of registration came back from COMELEC. Side one is the campaign: stump speeches layered with brass and snare, rice poured into a tin can for percussion, and a chorus of public-school teachers from Palauig singing the party hymn in three-part harmony. Side two is the province after dark — tricycle engines at the Masinloc market, fishing-boat generators humming in Santa Cruz, and Governor Deloso's own voice at 11:47 p.m. in the executive lounge, reading the constitution into a dictaphone because the studio had been booked by a wedding band.

One thousand copies, pressed in black vinyl; three hundred in citrus-yellow, for the citrus country. The sleeve was printed in Iba and jacketed against the monsoon. Play loud, and keep the needle clean.
