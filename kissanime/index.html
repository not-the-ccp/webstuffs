Elsewhere in the trade papers it was recorded as "one of the world's biggest streaming anime websites," which is a shorter way of saying the same thing and the office prefers it.

4First aid measures

If streamed: close the tab; the substance does not follow the tab, but the next episode is one click away and the office has watched it happen, so close the tab and get up. If ingested in quantity — a whole season, sitting down: the office records the condition as a full evening with a light on in the room and nothing else done; no antidote is prescribed, and none is needed, because the effect wears off the way weather does. If a download completes: keep the file where it is, tell no one, and remember that a file kept is a file the office will never have to explain. Main symptoms: the phrase "just one more episode," which the office classifies as a symptom and not a plan. Do not ring the switchboard on account of a download; the switchboard has its own queue.

5Firefighting measures

The substance does not burn; it is what catches. To put out a mirror, the office's standing order is: pull the link before you touch the wire, because a mirror pulled by its wire goes down with the wire attached and the record gets muddied. Do not throw water at the server room; the room is in the valley, the valley is low, and the valley has had worse weather. Fire fighters are asked to wear boots, to keep their names out of the report, and to remember that the thing they are standing in front of is, in the language of the law, a website, which is a word that has always sounded like it should be somewhere else.

6Accidental release measures

If an episode is released to the public without the rights holder's hand on the gate — and the office will note that the substance's whole business was releases of exactly this kind, in the ordinary course — the standing order is to contain, count, and not look. Contain the link. Count the views, because the counting is the evidence and the evidence must be a number. Do not look, because the picture will be good and the picture was good, which is why it moved, and the office has found that looking at it makes the report softer than it should be.

7Handling and storage

Handle with gloves if the gloves are for the job; the substance is a website and the gloves do nothing, but the habit is good. The mirror set was stored at the far rack of the relay office's valley room, in numbered boxes, the boxes on the pallet and the pallet on the flagstone, so that the damp that comes up off the valley floor would not reach the labels. Keep away from the licence desks, which are on the fourth floor and take the stairs the long way; the substance has never once come up the stairs by itself, and the office does not intend to test whether it still will not.

8Exposure controls / personal protection

No occupational limit has been set, because the audience — millions, per the report pinned above the switchboard — exceeded any limit the office could have set, and the exposure was the exposure. Operators at the relay were issued the standard kit, which was a lamp, a ledger, and the instruction to write down what they saw. No filter is effective against a moving picture; the office has tried, and the filter was a curtain, and the curtain is still there, and it has not filtered anything since 2019.

10Stability and reactivity

Stable under all conditions at which it was normally kept, which were the conditions of the open web, all of them, night and day. Incompatible with a seizure warrant, with a cease and desist, and with the one name of a rights holder that was on the fourth-floor list in red ink. Does not polymerize. Does not precipitate. Reacts, on contact with a mirror, by multiplying, and the office keeps the multiplication figure in a separate ledger, because that ledger is the one the lawyers will read first and the cobbler will never read at all.

11Toxicological information

Acute effects are those of a single episode, recorded above under first aid, and are fully reversible in the morning. Chronic effects, from the season long exposure the office's own switchboard operators gave themselves in the quiet hours, are listed in the medical book as the habit of the free frame: the expectation, seated and permanent, that the moving picture should cost nothing, which the office has found does not reverse in the morning, and which outlasts the substance itself, which is the finding the sheet exists to put on paper. No death, no illness, no hospital: the toxicity of the substance runs through the licence line and nowhere else, and the licence line is a long line, per section 2.

12Ecological information

Mobile by link, stationary by mirror, the substance colonized the anime shelf of the open web the way a river colonizes a valley: along the low ground, around the obstacles, and past the locks that were built for it, because the locks were built for a smaller water. It outcompeted the licensed species for the evening hour, which is the only hour the licensed species has, and it did so at a price of nothing, which is the price the office's instruments could not register and the fourth floor could. The field note on the ecology is one line, entered under the month the report was pinned up: the river does not argue with the lock, and the river does not stop at it either.

13Disposal considerations

The domain, when it was handed over, went to the authority that asked for it, which is the only disposal a website of this class receives and the office will not embellish. The mirrors went to the sea, as per the instruction in section 2, and the sea keeps them, and the sea does not bill. The sister substance, KissManga, was found to be keeping to itself after the seizure; the office has not opened its card since, and will not, because a sister that is keeping to itself is a sister that has learned something from the other sister's ledger, and the office does not read its students' homework aloud.

14Transport information

No UN number, no placard, no placard possible: the substance travels by fibre, which the transport codes do not cover, because the codes were written for things that weigh. For road, river and rail the declared quantity is one pipe in the wall and a lamp in the valley room, and both are declared here for the record, because the record is the office's whole business and a pipe in the wall is a fact of the same class as any other. Sea transport is not applicable. Air is not applicable, and the office asks that the two non-applicabilities not be read as the office having opinions on the weather.

15Regulatory information

Not authorized in any jurisdiction in which it operated, which was the open web, which is all of them; the violation, per the entry under class in section 1, is the fact of the free frame, and the free frame is not a condition that can be cured with a permit. Entered in the office's record at relay book entry 1417, and re-entered at each revision, which has made seven. No entry in the waste lists, for the same reason the moth of the Para River sheet has none: the substance does not become waste. It becomes a mirror, and the mirrors are kept by the sea, and the sea is regulated by no one at 1417 Canal Street.

16Other information

In the relay book, page 214, in the hand of the operator who sat the chair on the night the seizure order came up the stairs, there is one line that the office transcribed without correction, because the office's rule is that a record is a record and the rule is not for argument. It reads, word for word:

The chair then read into the North End In the valley without trouble, let this one sends you south first.

The office's marginalia, on the back of the page, gives the operator's own gloss, in smaller ink: the chair is the operator's chair; the North End is this office; the valley is the low route down Canal Street where the damp comes up and the mirrors were kept; the "one" is the last mirror, boxed and numbered, and the order, whatever else it is, is to send it south first, before the fourth floor sends for it, which the fourth floor would have done, which it did not, and which the operator recorded by writing the sentence twice, once for the book and once for the box's lid. The mirror in question went south. The office has not learned where it stopped, and has entered the matter under Other, which is where it keeps the things it is not ready to explain.
