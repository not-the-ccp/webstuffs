Biome

A distinct geographical region with specific climate, vegetation, and animal life; a biological community formed in response to its physical environment and regional climate. Advertisers may refer to the definition, which is entered under the heading Biome and is not for argument.

For Sale by Descent · No. 1

One tropical rainforest biome, lowland, slightly north of the equator, the Atlantic running into the tree line. From the estate of the Republic of Suriname, who died at home in Paramaribo, 14 May 2024 — see the obituary on page B4, entered beside the safety data sheet. The property stands in an area of approximately 165,940 square kilometres, of which over 95 per cent was canopy: kapok and wallaba, the morning howler chorus, and the rivers that rise in the wet months and never quite recede in the memory of the people who live beside them. The estate's neighbours — Guyana to the west, Brazil to the south, French Guiana to the east, the Atlantic to the north — hold first refusal, and would have attended the graveside, had anyone known where to dig. No flowers; tenders are asked to bring a lamp at forty watts, left burning, and one window open toward the rain. Interment beneath the canopy; the forest itself is not sold, only its description, which is what a biome is in the first place.

Ref. PF-95 · Para River Entomological Stores, No. 14 Kruiskade

For Let · No. 2

One ecosystem, established 1935, to let furnished. The premises were formerly part of the idea of a biome; in 1935, Arthur Tansley added the climatic and soil aspects to the idea, and calling it ecosystem. A suitable biological community is invited to form in response to the physical environment and regional climate provided. The rent is a description; the lease is for as long as the climate holds. Previous tenants include the lowland forest, the dry season, and the old guilder, the last two having predeceased the notice. Quiet occupation; howlers permitted at dawn.

Ref. TA-1935 · apply to the clerk, who reads the dark the way other animals read the light

Wanted: Field Crews · No. 3

The International Biological Program (1964–74) seeks surveyors, one per region, to popularize the concept of biome. The work is the walk and the counting: a distinct geographical region, specific climate, vegetation, animal life, entered as found. Pay is the description itself, kept word for word in the program's ledger, which the ledger of the lower room, N'Djamena, has inspected and finds acceptable: it is all water, it is all true, and it is impossible to amend. The program's ten years ended, but the walks have not, and a field crew from the Aegean, keeping tide and weather from a gulf between two peninsulas, reports the measures hold at Güllük Gulf — Field Notes. Applications close at the last flood, which is not for argument.

Ref. IBP-74 · answer in two official languages where two apply

To Let, in German Measure · No. 4

One biotope, a concrete geographical unit, after the Walter terminology, to let to a tenant who wants the word as German literature keeps it: particular, fixed, with one door. Tenants preferring the measure used in this office — the international, non-regional biome, which names a kind of place rather than a parcel of it — may apply in duplicate, and both entries will be kept, because in some contexts the term is used in a different manner and a good notice-keeper is not in the business of choosing. The same word does both jobs here; no double charge, and no argument, on account of the rule about names that is kept in every register from No. 14 Kruiskade to the riverside building in N'Djamena, where the last of the tea was poured over the table and the table survived the pour, as recorded in the Minutes of the Council, Session I.

Ref. W-2 · the name, if it is a name, survives the pour

Lost · No. 5

Lost from the galley of the boat that is not in the water: one marlingspike, whalebone, slightly worn, last seen in the cedar box that carried the forty-watt lamp from No. 14 Kruiskade to the lower room and back. A letter from the last keeper of the galley is filed with this notice and entered exactly as read, the notice-keeper having corrected nothing and not being, in any case, the one to whom the letter was addressed: “Making coffee in filters or percolaters does away entirely with the marlingspike—“that is the hinge, and that whale, Moby Dick. The line is kept for the hinge, of which the council has one on retainer in N'Djamena, and for the whale, of whom the lake at the edge of the map, which belongs to four countries and to the wind and has said nothing all year, may yet have a sounding. Reward: one description, word for word, of the region the spake was lost in. The boatman, whose reason is the boat, is asked to attend.

Ref. MS-1 · found folded in the notice book? enter under Unattributed

Public Notice · No. 6

NOTICE IS HEREBY given that, on account of the flood, the Council of Rivers, Borders & Names, Republic of Chad, landlocked and fifth-largest in Africa, an area of around 1,300,000 square kilometres with twenty million and two official languages and a door on no side of the hexagon, has re-entered Lake Chad in its register as a wetland biome, the entry standing in the drawer with the lamp at forty watts, the obituary clipping from this newspaper, and the pinned moth of the family Erebidae described by Caspar Stoll in 1781 from Surinamese material, Cosmosoma evadnes, whole, pinned, patient. The measurement is deferred to the flood, which is deferred to the river, which keeps its own calendar and has not yet given a date. Persons hearing the lake say anything at all are asked to notify the clerk of the lower room, formerly of the Para River Entomological Stores, who has been across two seas and one obituary and would consider the news a record, and a record is a record.

Ref. LC-0 · filed in both official languages and in the water

Notice-keeper's Note

The desk reminds advertisers that the term biome is entered in this office in the international, non-regional manner: a distinct geographical region with specific climate, vegetation, and animal life, not a single parcel of ground, which is the German usage and is kept under its own head, biotope, under Ref. W-2. The desk was opened beside the entry book that received the late Republic of Suriname on 14 May 2024 and the living Republic of Chad at its first session, and it holds no form for the living, which the clerk has observed is the true test of a country: not whether it will die, but whether the clerk has a form. Lines are charged by the word; descriptions are charged by the region; and where any name happens to be ready for use, pour over the table. The science which gave this page its heading — ecology, from the Greek word for house, which is what No. 14 Kruiskade is, and has always been — died at its bench on 14 May 2024, and her paid notice stands on page B4 beside the Republic's, entered in the same stroke: Ecology — Obituary.
