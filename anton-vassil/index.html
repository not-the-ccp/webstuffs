The Cutting Room Diary
14 March 2019 · Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire

Dear diary,

I came to Abidjan for Le Gendarme de Abobo, Vassil's latest, shooting in the Abobo district with a crew half French, half Ivorian, release pencilled for 2019 — or so the notice said, a single typed sheet pinned above the production desk that smelled of diesel and photocopier toner. Anton Vassil. I first met his name in a Loyola Marymount catalogue, a master's graduate of the film school who cut his teeth on Marching Out of Time, a first feature shot in the Mojave with a cast of friends and a camera that overheated by noon. From there: Guderian, a study of the tank commander in raincoats and archive footage; La Dictature de la Pensée Unique, "the dictatorship of single thought," briefly banned somewhere I never learned; Les Oiseaux, not Hitchcock but something quieter, about birds in a provincial town.

In 2008 he developed ISS Space Agency, a European thriller set in a control room outside Cologne, all blue light and headsets. Then Laurent et Safi, a French musical feature shot in 2015 and released theatrically in France and Africa in 2017 — cinemas in Dakar, Abidjan, Kinshasa, songs recorded in a studio on the Rue des Écoles. I saw a print last week; the colour had faded in exactly the way he wanted, "like a memory of sunlight," he told me.

Today the post brought another notice from the East Midlands before they crack open — five reels of Marching Out of Time from a lab in Leicester, seals intact, cans stamped 1984. I will watch them tonight and write down what remains of Vassil's first film before the print shrinks any further.

At the counter, a local paper, bought for the weather page: page 7 is a set of classifieds for a wrestling tag team, Well Dunn, a.k.a. the Southern Rockers — Timothy Well and Steven Dunn, a pun on "well done," four belts and one tour bus, and a weather desk that counts the seconds between lightning and thunder. I clipped the page. It goes in the drawer with the production notices, at The Arena & Rundown, Classifieds, Page 7.

— A.
