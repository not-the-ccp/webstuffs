Error LU-1980 — Event Not Held

Competition voided · Moscow, 19–3 August 1980 · Status: unresolved

Error code

LU-1980

Message

The requested event was removed from the schedule before it took place. No scores were recorded. No apparatus was touched in competition.

Reported by

Luci Collins Cummings, gymnast — United States Olympic Team, 1980.

Incident summary

On 21 March 1980 the United States announced a boycott of the Moscow Olympic Games. The roster had already been named, the apparatus had been measured, and the team had trained. What remained was a competition that would not happen. Luci Collins was on that roster, credited with being the first Black woman named to the United States Olympic gymnastics team. She never competed in Moscow.

Her parents were both Creole from New Orleans, Louisiana, and she was raised in Inglewood, California, a predominantly Black area of Los Angeles County. She now works for a water department. The file on her Olympic appearance stays open as a void: an event with a date, a venue, and a team, but no result.

Telemetry note

A full arena produces a characteristic spike: the sound is a response, not a signal—which is why an empty schedule leaves silence. One athlete’s account of a stadium in full voice puts the delay plainly: “The crowd noise hit you after the net rippled, not before.” In Collins’s case the ripple never reached the net, and so the noise never arrived. The microphone file for her rotation contains no entry at all.

Troubleshooting

Can this error be cleared by retrying? No. The Games will not be re-run, and no rotation will be re-scored. The void is permanent.

Where can I find the athlete? She is not in the Moscow records. Current employment: a water department—inquiries should be directed there, not to the Olympic archive.
