Abstract of the Disclosure

The invention relates to sustained televisual presence, and in particular to a presence that is produced not by a single individual acting alone but by a familial production lineage operating as one apparatus. The inventor, Geoffrey Michael Alatiit Eigenmann (Tagalog: [𝕒&icaron;ðnmæn]; born 23 March 1985, the date on which this specification is deemed filed in the log’s reckoning), is a Filipino actor and model who is notable for his performances in television. He is a member of the Eigenmann family of actors and a son of the actor and director Michael De Mesa. The apparatus of the invention comprises a son, a father, a surname, a television schedule, and a guide; and the method comprises the repeated performance, on camera and off, of the same recognisable person, such that a viewer may identify the presence at forty paces and a second generation’s notice.

Detailed Description — the specification in full, as summarised

[0001] The present invention is an improvement upon a prior art consisting of the Eigenmann family of actors, a lineage of performers in which several generations have held televisual presence under one surname. The problem the invention addresses is this: how does a presence, once held, remain held across a birth, a first audition, and a first credit, without lapsing into the state of a presence that is merely inherited and never demonstrated?

[0002] According to the present invention, the presence is held by way of a son — Geoffrey Michael Alatiit Eigenmann, actor and model — operating in conjunction with his father, the actor and director Michael De Mesa, the presence’s upstream component. The father supplies the apparatus with its original calibration: diction, posture, and a working knowledge of the camera at working distance. The son supplies the renewal. Both are named in the specification because a lineage that omits either component is a lineage that has broken.

[0003] The method further comprises the following steps, in order: (a) the inventor is born, 23 March 1985, with a surname already in production; (b) the inventor performs, in television, such that his performances become the basis on which he is notable; (c) the inventor models, in the interim, so that the presence is exercised in stillness as well as in speech; and (d) the inventor remains a member of the family, which is to say, the presence is maintained under collective ownership of the Eigenmann name.

[0004] A preferred embodiment of the apparatus includes a guide. The guide is not an actor. The guide is the person who, at field demonstrations of the present invention, points the audience to the place where the presence is actually being held, and who reads aloud, at the appointed turn, the lines the audience is to take away. Field demonstrations of the present invention are staged at the coalition walk, of which a record is kept elsewhere on this site in the Coalition Walk — Field Record, at the fourth glass along the line. The transcript of the demonstrations is preserved verbatim in the record, examiner’s hand, without annotation:

Turn four times, at the next glass of the coalition, the guide will point out that coalitions under proportional systems, where party lists split the Brassicaceae twice in one place.

Field transcript, demonstration No. 2, 23 March, entered verbatim at the appointed turn. The record closes the entry without translation.

[0005] The examiner notes, for the completeness of the record, that the reference to the Brassicaceae in the field transcript is understood in the present invention to designate the mustard bed at the demonstration ground, which the party lists — two, of four and four — do in fact split twice in one place, first by the path and then by the water line. The bed itself, its genus and its habit, is surveyed elsewhere in the archive at The Mustard Walk — Field Notes, and the reader is referred to that record for the botany, which the present invention does not claim.

[0006] The advantages of the present invention over the prior art are as follows: the presence is continuous across generations; it is notable, which is to say, it is noticed by the public and recorded; it is exercised in two media (television and model work) and therefore does not depend on a single schedule; and it is held under a surname — Eigenmann, pronounced as the Tagalog rendering indicates, [aopf;&icaron;ðnmæn] — that functions as a serial number the audience can carry away.

Claims

  1. Claim 1. A method for sustaining televisual presence in a familial production lineage, comprising: (a) a first member, an actor and director, named Michael De Mesa; (b) a second member, a son of the first, named Geoffrey Michael Alatiit Eigenmann, an actor and model; and (c) the repeated performance, by the second member, in television, such that the presence remains held and is notable.
  2. Claim 2. The method of Claim 1, wherein the second member’s performances in television are the basis on which he is notable, and wherein the presence is maintained without lapse through the interval between performances.
  3. Claim 3. The method of Claim 1, further comprising a guide at a field demonstration, the guide reading at the appointed turn a line of the field transcript, entered verbatim in the record without annotation.
  4. Claim 4. An apparatus comprising a surname, a birth entry dated 23 March 1985, a television schedule, and a camera at working distance, the apparatus being held under collective ownership of the Eigenmann family of actors.
  5. Claim 5. The apparatus of Claim 4, wherein the surname functions as a serial number, rendered in Tagalog as [aopf;&icaron;ðnmæn], and wherein the serial number is carried away by the audience.
  6. Claim 6. A demonstration, staged at the fourth glass of the coalition walk, wherein the presence is pointed out to the audience by the guide, and wherein the mustard bed is split twice in one place by two party lists.
  7. Claim 7. The method of Claim 1, wherein the first member and the second member are named in the specification, and wherein the lineage that omits either is a lineage that has broken.
