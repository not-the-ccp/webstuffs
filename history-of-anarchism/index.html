IN MEMORIAM

HISTORY OF ANARCHISM

c. 500 BC – 2024 AD · died without an exact date, as had been feared

Survived by a range of views on what it means, which is the only thing that survived anything, cleanly.

History of anarchism died on a Tuesday, the way large ambiguous things die, in the reading room of the Department of Ambiguous Histories, where its file — four crates, one of them damp — had been kept since the 1872 Hague Congress. Whether the death is to be dated to some hour in the nineteenth century, when the movement became formal enough to be a movement at all, or to some moment before the earliest civilisations, when its traits were already present in the cave of the argument, the scholars disagree, and the funeral was scheduled for both. Attendance was accordingly split.

Those who came to the early service held that anarchism is a distinct, well-defined movement stemming from 19th-century class conflict, and they arrived in coats. Those who came to the late one held that its roots go back to ancient and prehistoric ideologies and social structures, and they arrived in sandals, or claimed they always had. The first traces of formal anarchist thought they could agree on sit in ancient Greece and ancient China, where numerous philosophers questioned the necessity of the state and declared the moral bankruptcy of compulsion: Pyrrho of Elis and the Skeptic school in the 4th century BC; Zhuangzi, who in the 4th century BC held that a well-ordered state is one so ordered that its people forget it is there; and Diogenes of Sinope, who in a barrel at the Agora performed the whole argument live, nightly, to an audience of lamps.

The modern body was given, in the view of the family, at William Godwin's press in London, 1793, with An Enquiry Concerning Political Justice, though the birth certificate has never been satisfactorily notarised. It grew up in two workshops. The first was the Lyon workshop of Pierre-Joseph Proudhon (1808–1865), compositor and keeper of the Journal du Peuple, who in 1840 answered the question Qu'est-ce que la propriété? with a number — theft — and is thus remembered as the first man to call himself an anarchist on the record. The second was the circle of Mikhail Bakunin, who in 1868 at the first congress of the International Workingmen's Association at Geneva said, in effect, that a free society will do the thinking for itself, and who split with Marx at the Hague in 1872 and was expelled, or expelled himself, depending on which ledger you trust. In Paris, during the Commune of 1871, the body ran wild in a good way: workshops reoccupied, the Bank of France left standing but watched, and the idea of a voluntary federation written on a wall in the Faubourg Saint-Antoine in letters a metre tall. In Chicago, on 4 May 1886, at Haymarket Square, the body was shot at by the crowd's own weather: an anonymous bomb, four police dead, the state's answer of eight men indicted, seven convicted, four hanged on 11 November 1887 and one, Adolph Fischer, dead in the cell the week before. The word anarchy has been pronounced in courts ever since, usually quietly.

It had a long second life: Peter Kropotkin (1842–1921) mapping mutual aid in the Siberian taiga and then in the 1902 pages of Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution; the cantonal communes of 1918–1921 in the Russian countryside; the collective farms and syndicated factories of the Aragon and the Levante, 1936–1939, which kept running without a boss at a time when the ledgers around them were all on fire. The last entry in its file is a range of views. Some scholars feel it a distinct movement; others identify its traits long before the earliest civilisations. The file, which had been impossible to order from the start, has now become impossible to close.

❧ THE LAST WORDS ❧

The Department asked Eustacia Vell, river-pilot of the Betwa for forty-one years and the only person who had ever read the file twice without argument, to report what the deceased seemed to want, if anything, after. She came to the reading room on a Thursday, set her oilskin coat on the table, and said, in the register of the Department, the following, which is entered here word for word, uncorrected:

I have taken on the Betwa and like nothing so much as a behind .

By the Betwa, those who knew her took her to mean the whole river trade she had run out of her office at Jhansi — sixty-one kilometres of the run she could pilot blind in fog, which she did, regularly, for the pleasure of it. By a behind, she meant the scow she had bought in 1989 for eleven thousand rupees and a pair of boots, the Behind — so called, she said, because it was always behind everything, and proud of it. She is alive; she pilots still; the Department has asked her to hold the file open.

The sentence is preserved exactly as entered, spacing and all. The Department does not correct its own river.

Funeral arrangements & cross-references from the shelf

Services will continue. Those wishing to keep a register of the movement as it actually moved — not as the file says it moved — will find the nearest kept accounts in two ledgers the Department keeps on its lower shelf. The first is the Ledger Leaf, No. 63, of the Mansfield Register Keepers, 1956, from the tea house on Elm Street, where a small club of eleven entered four petitions to a school board in ink and defended a boy who read registers back to himself until the order of the thing was the only furniture in the room; the file of the deceased spent, by some accounts, its years 1872–1954 in exactly that kind of room. The second is Almanac Entry No. 217, Berlin Station, January 5–12, 1919, which reads the Spartacist uprising as a pressure trough and logs its heaviest fighting as fair weather at the daily minimum; the deceased, had it been able to see the weather sheet, would have approved of the method and disapproved of the outcome, in that order.

Memorial contributions may be made to the repair of the press room at Lyon, to the lamp at the Agora barrel, or to the eleven thousand rupees owed the Behind, still outstanding since 1989. In lieu of flowers, an argument: bring one, hold it, do not resolve it. The file remains open at the front page of the archive, where it has always been, in the middle of the shelf, out of the reach of anyone who had intended to close it.
