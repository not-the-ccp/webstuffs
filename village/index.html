The Village

Gornje Vukovsko · Sana ravine · Municipality of Kupres · Canton 10, Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina

Kupres municipal office → the drainage-channel track → Trgovina Kovač → the stone wall → the Sana · 3.2 km up, 4.1 km back with detours

On the scale of the geographer

The textbooks are agreeable on one point and that is the point to start from, if you are keeping a guide: a village is a human settlement or residential community, larger than a hamlet but smaller than a town, and some geographers describe villages as having between 500 and 2,500 inhabitants. On that scale the valley must be entered with a footnote. The last full count put Gornje Vukovsko at 174 persons, in 58 dwellings, counted in November, which is the wrong month, since half the men were down in Kupres for the market and the count of the sheep was left to the owner. The dwellings are fairly close to one another — you can call across every one of them — and not scattered broadly over the landscape, as a dispersed settlement would be; the farthest house, the one with the green door and the dead well, sits only eleven minutes from the shop. It is permanent, with fixed dwellings, although the ravine has its one transient village as well: the summer counting camp of the three families who drive their flocks up to the high meadows below Vranjani in June and out again by the first frost. In the old days, when the valley ran entirely on subsistence agriculture — maize on the terraces, orchards on the south face, the Sana doing the milling — Gornje Vukovsko was the usual form of community, and a whole society turned on it; the guide's advice is to walk it in November, when the terraces are bare and the whole arrangement of the place is plain to see.

Geographers will notice the valley's population falls short of their lower figure and are asked to notice, also, that the term urban village is applied by the same discipline to certain urban neighbourhoods, so that the word already does two jobs. The valley assigns it a third, and the guide records it here rather than argues it: the village, in the Sana ravine, is whatever the parcel post will reach.

Getting there

From the municipal office at Radna cesta 12, Kupres 83273 — the office whose cabinet 9 we shall not open for you, we are sorry — take the valley road to the crossing where the old Kupres copper-mine drainage channel meets the pavement, and leave the pavement. The track climbs beside the channel for 3.2 kilometres. It is level for most of the way; the climb is only in the mind. There is one bench, a stone one, no back, at the place where the track narrows between two walnut trees. Allow forty minutes up, and take the return with the light going down the ravine, which is the better showing. No vehicle has taken the track since 1994, when a tractor went in for the terraces and the terraces said no; the guide records the event because the valley records its events, and because the ruts are still visible in October.

Stops

No. 1 · Trgovina Kovač, the shop

The shop is the first building on the track and the last institution of the valley to have a telephone; the telephone, we are told, is in the back room and the back room is for the telephone. Notices are pinned beside the parcel post — the lost-and-found notice no. 14/2024, concerning the vellum leaf found below the wall, is pinned there beside the parcel post, and has been for a season; the shopkeeper pins things and unpins nothing, which is a municipal principle carried home. Coffee is strong, conversation is slower. The shop keeps a visitor book, one page per year, and the guide's notes for the following section are drawn from it.

No. 2 · The stone wall, the ravine

Below the track, where the drainage channel crosses the road down to Donje Vukovsko, a low stone wall runs along the edge of the Sana ravine. It is the wall the finder had been counting his ewes above — Idriz Hodžić, shepherd, 67, of Gornje Vukovsko, forty-one ewes, none lost in the finding — and it was on this wall, in a waxed-paper envelope inside a rusted oilcan, that the vellum leaf was set down on the afternoon of 3 October 2024. The wall itself is an unremarkable wall of the right height, built of the field stones of the terraces, but it is where the leaf came out of the ravine, and a guide has a duty of directions. The notice, the register of claims, the reward of 300 convertible marks, and the drawer of the lock that no longer locks are all at Lost & Found — A Vellum Leaf, Gornje Vukovsko, filed in the same municipal office the track begins at; the two documents are a day apart in the valley's reckoning and a wall apart in the world.

No. 3 · The Sana, at the ravine

The Sana here is the width of a good road and the temper of the valley. In autumn it runs the colour the tempera of the leaf's initials went, which is the comparison the valley itself makes and the guide does not improve on. The river does the milling the textbooks remember; the mill that did it is a foundation and a rumor, but the channel bed of the old wheel is stoned into the bank and can be read in a dry October. Wading is not recommended and has never once been needed, which is the whole of the swimming information.

No. 4 · The stewpan, for the evening

The Sana stewpan kitchen sets twenty-eight tables on the far bank, and a reader of the route journal knows already that its chef copied one brown line from the verso of the leaf into the left margin of his menu above the prices; the menu has not since been changed, and neither, it is said, has the stew. The table review is kept at Dining Across the Meridian — a table review of the Sana stewpan. For the traveller: take the walk up in the morning, the bench at the walnuts at noon, the ravine in the going-down light, and the stewpan at dusk, and you will have the whole village in one day without once having explained it.

From the visitor book, Trgovina Kovač

The book takes one page per year and has taken, in the hand of a reader from the north who came up with a theodolite and left it on the bench at the walnuts — it is still there — the following. The shopkeeper reads the book as the office reads the ravine: as the text first stands.

“Let her conduct that its memory is not necessary so completely backwards that only calls forth life, but gives it a nice feeder.”

Visitor book, Trgovina Kovač · the autumn of the leaf's finding · theodolite left

Read the way the valley reads it, and the sentence is a set of directions. Her is the Sana; the river conducts the village the way a track conducts a traveller — level for most of the way, the climb only in the mind. The memory of the place, the line goes, is not necessary so completely backwards that it only calls forth life: it is not a looking-back that summons the village into being. It gives it a nice feeder — and the valley has one, a small spring stream that joins the Sana above the wall and runs all the way up to the terraces, which is what a feeder is, and what the memory of the place, read forward, turns out to be: water coming down to where the dwellings sit close together. The theodolite's owner never came back for the instrument, the shopkeeper pins and unpins nothing, and the line stands in the book exactly as it was set down, which is the whole of the village's practice, and the guide's only advice on the matter.

Further reading
