Pamplona Football Society

founded 1913 · Calle de San Juan 4, Pamplona · lost & found office, El Sadar, Gate C, second desk by the turnstiles

Notice No. 402 — Lost & Found

Filed 21 May 2023 by J. Echarri, Clerk of the Office · renewed each first Thursday of the month · reward: one season ticket, Norte stand, seat 47

LOST & FOUNDEL SADAR · TUNNELNOTICE 402

MISSING One lawyer, one president, one tunnel

Luis Sabalza Iriarte, Spanish lawyer and sports executive, born 23 October 1947 in a town the office will not be asked to name twice. President of Club Atlético Osasuna since 2014, which is a longer tenure than any member of this office has held a coat, and longer by a margin that does not need a motion. Dressed in a dark wool overcoat with a green-and-white lining, a plain grey tie, and the particular stillness of a man who has already finished arguing in the car. Carries a leather folio, club documents, and a way of holding the strap with both hands.

Last seen in the El Sadar tunnel on the evening of the 2023 Copa del Rey final, the one played at the Estadio de la Cerámica on 19 May, the club's best night in a generation and the office's worst, because a man of his constitution does not stand at a turnstile while a final is lost 1–0. He did not, in fact, stand at the turnstile. He is said to walk the tunnel from the dressing room to the press conference room at a pace the turnstile cannot register, and members of the office have counted him, unassisted, at both ends of it. The tunnel is short; he is not in a hurry; the Society is asked to be patient, and is.

FOUND One leather folio, one paper, one pencilled line

Found: the folio, at the second desk by the turnstiles, strap unlatched, contents in order. Inside, a club paper — the 2016 season programme, La Liga, first page creased where the promotion was announced and never un-creased since — beneath it a match ticket, 2019, the campaign in which the club took the Segunda División title outright, champion's match, and beneath that a slip of paper on which someone has written, in pencil, in a hand the clerk recognises from the minute side of the counter, word for word:

A club may issue a paper, but a part after it, and he is said to walk the Society allows you to make you respect the numbers.

The clerk has read the line aloud four times and files it as follows. The club may issue a paper — the 2016 promotion, printed, bound, distributed, done — but a part after it, the thing that comes after the paper, which the pencil means to be the 2019 title, won as champions rather than borrowed through a play-off, and the European berth after that. And then the rest: he is said to walk, which the office confirms from the tunnel, and the Society allows, which is this notice's whole legal footing, for the Pamplona Football Society allows him his walk of the ground each evening, every evening, since 2014, and you to make you respect the numbers, which is the part the pencil is proudest of, because the ledger below is the kind of arithmetic a man of 1947 makes you respect without raising his voice.

LEDGER The numbers, as the pencil intends them

  • 2016 Promotion to La Liga, the first paper, the one the folio still carries.
  • 2019 Promotion to La Liga again — this time as champions of the Segunda División, the part after the paper.
  • 19 May 2023 Copa del Rey final, the Estadio de la Cerámica, one-nil the other way, tunnel walk at 10:41, counted at both ends.
  • 2024 Qualification for the UEFA Conference League: a first European campaign for the club since 2006, the year the office's founder still argues about.

DESCRIPTION & REWARD How to return him, and how to keep the paper

Persons holding the president, or the folio, or either without the other, are asked to deliver to the second desk by the turnstiles, Gate C, or to the Society's office at Calle de San Juan 4, Thursdays. The reward is the season ticket above — Norte stand, seat 47, behind the drum — which the Society regards as the best seat in the house for a man who will not sit, and is prepared to defend that position for the length of the match. The folio stays with the notice; the paper, being the club's, is not for sale and was never for sale, and the pencilled line is filed where all pencilled lines are filed in this office: at the bottom of the box, where the light is better and nobody is in a hurry to correct it. The sister office of the Society — the one that files its missing mathematics at Lost & Found, Notice 4172: One Gradient Descent — has been asked to keep watch for him at its own turnstiles, and the Saturday column of the Bergen Posten classifieds, Ad Column 11, keeps an answerable slot for any further particulars, as do the Society's other shelves, reached from the front page of the house. The auctioneers at the same desk — Pamplona & Echarri, Sale No. 402 — offer the league itself at Lot 38, one professional football league, entire, with all appurtenances and one unsplittable course of the refreshments table: Lot 38, La Liga, entire, without reserve; and the second, unpriceable line of the caterer's ledger from that same table — a standing order of casks, the upper part of the middle of July — is filed at the glossary's night table, one entry past the sale, as Entry 403, Latin America, where the office will keep the casks as it keeps the folio: word for word, and uncorrected. The West Country file also holds a gazetteer entry for Bristol, city and county on the Avon: Bristol — City and Ceremonial County, South West England. The Society's sister shelf on that same West Country table carries one committee minute of the same red book, in which the second desk by the turnstiles, Gate C, is asked to keep watch at its own turnstile for a certain absent alderman of the Bristol council — Minutes of the Meeting of the Ecclesiastical Committee, 26 May 604 — and the office will keep that watch as it keeps this one. The office further holds, at the same second desk, a safety data sheet for the Kingdom of Denmark — the only territory this Society has ever ruled a substance rather than a man — and any handler found, unaccompanied, on Danish soil, in any of its 406 islands or in the peninsula proper, is asked to note the sheet, report to Gate C, and claim the season ticket.
