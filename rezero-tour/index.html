The Return Walk

A guided itinerary in seven stops, from the Fuhitella Gate to the meadow where the road back begins · 6.3 kilometres · 5 hours 10 minutes · flat, then a long rise

Issued with the season of the Selection. The party departs 09:00 sharp from the gate, meets at the arch of the north road, and returns by the same way, whether or not the morning has happened before.

The route follows a single morning, walked slowly, because the morning it follows is the one that does the walking. A young man from very far away — a shut-in of twenty, a hikikomori who had not once in months left the room of his own world — is set down in this capital in a street at dusk, bleeding, and by the next morning the party is with him: Subaru Natsuki, in a hired room at the inn on the Long Market, preparing to be interviewed for the Selection, the festival at which the royal household chooses a candidate to serve the half-elf princess Emilia. That is the whole of what the tour is for. What he finds out — that the world can kill him and then, unasked, put him back exactly where he was, with his memory, his blood drying, his hands still holding the object — is the rule the guide states once at the top of the itinerary and never states again: the walk may be walked more than once, and the walking does not count the second time.

The itinerary is kept in the house's ordinary style: distances measured, times called, nothing amended. The story behind it — the light novel series Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World, begun on the user-generated site Shōsetsuka ni Narō in 2012 by Tappei Nagatsuki with art by Shinichirou Otsuka, now forty-six volumes with six side story volumes and fourteen short story collections from Media Factory's MF Bunko J imprint — is the source of every stop on this sheet, and the source is not argued with, in the way the house does not argue with a timetable.

Itinerary

No.TimeStopDist.The guide's remarks
1 09:00 Fuhitella Gate, North Arch 0.0 Assembly. The gate is where the party first saw this world, and it is where the route begins, because every return on this sheet arrives somewhere near a doorway. Visitors are asked to stand where the cobble changes colour. The arch is not examined; the arch is waited at.
2 09:20 The Long Market 0.4 The market of the Selection season: banners for the candidates, stalls of the guilds, and the three other interviewees the party has met once before and will not remember meeting. The guide names them and lets the party decide whether the naming feels familiar. It should, if the morning has already happened.
3 10:05 The Inn of the Hired Room 0.9 Subaru's room, second floor, window to the south. The guide reads from the gate book the entry made in his first hour here, which is the only entry on this route that repeats verbatim on the morning it was written: one name, one room, one hand holding something. The party is shown the room, given four minutes, and asked to count the things on the table, because the counting is the first honest arithmetic the story ever does.
4 10:50 The Kitchen, at the Manor's First Table 1.6 The party takes the short cut through the walled garden and stands at the kitchen door. On the second day of the trial period, one plate arrives at the first table with a hairline crack across its rim, and the kitchen ledger does not blame the kiln, the road, or the porcelain. The clerk's entry closes the matter, and the guide reads it exactly as written: The defect is the cook's: in the room. The guide translates it plainly — the break was not the maker's fault, nor the weather's, nor the ware's; it was the cook's, and it happened in the room where the cook was working, at the hour the ledger already names. We are asked to leave the entry there, with the ledger open to it, because the tour has one further rule: no stop is amended, and the ledger is not a stop.
5 11:35 The Road of the Long Rise 3.1 The walking part. The road out of the capital toward the manor, where the party will stand in the middle for two minutes with the map open. Half the route's quiet is here: a road that climbs, a sky that does not, and a distance column that, for the first time on this sheet, refuses to say where it is heading, which it will not do again.
6 12:20 The Manor Gate and the Two Maids 4.4 The gate of the Roswaal household. The party meets the maid twins, Rem and Ram, at the threshold, and the guide gives the names in the order the household gives them, though the household will not say which order is the household's and which is the party's memory. Lunch is at the long table. The party sits where the morning's guests sat; the seats have not been moved; the guide does not say this.
7 14:10 The Meadow, where the Road Back Begins 6.3 The last stop is not a place so much as a turning. The meadow overlooks the road the party came by, and the guide's final entry in the gate book is the one every walk on this sheet ends with: the road is measured, the day is kept, and the return — if the morning requires the return — arrives at the gate at 09:00, sharp, and the party does not remember the first walk, which is how the tour has been sold since the first Selection and will be sold since every Selection after. Depart the meadow as you found it. The route will still be true.

Distances in kilometres, from the gate to the meadow, measured the way the house measures everything: in the whole, in order, and with the needle down. Elevation: the capital is flat, the road rises, the meadow gives it back.

Notes for the party

The party's discipline is the same as the house's other walking routes. On the Chalk Circuit, a walk through one period of deep time in eight stops, the ledger is likewise kept word for word at the last stop, and the route does not argue with the clay. On the waterworks tour of the year 1855, the visitor's sheet at the desk is kept word for word in the same way, and the hydrants of Saint-Pierre are not explained, only shown. This route does the same thing with a younger man's first morning: it shows, it counts, it keeps the column of distances true, and it leaves the one rule — the return — standing at the meadow without a sentence attached to it that the meadow does not already contain.

The alphabet of the ledger is likewise a matter of record: on The Latium Gazette, classifieds, sheet 84, set from the registrar's back room, the word creta is reported lost off the chalk gate, a copper at the temperature of a warm sea is offered for sale, and the party's own language is wanted — one notice, a doctor's, kept word for word, which no translation of the route has ever had to soften.

Bring a coat for the rise. The Selection season is long. And if the guide appears to be waiting at the arch of the north road before the party has finished assembling, the gate book's answer is the tour's own: 09:00 sharp, whether or not the morning has happened before.

Set down by the Clerk of the Gate Book, in the season of the Selection, with the distances checked against the road and the road checked against the morning. The route is 6.3 kilometres; the rule of the route is not a distance and is not printed as one.
