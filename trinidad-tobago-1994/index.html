About this set

Trinidad and Tobago sent a delegation to the 1994 Winter Olympics in Lillehammer, Norway, from 12–27 February 1994 — the country’s first appearance at a Winter Games. The delegation consisted of two bobsledders, Gregory Sun and Curtis Harry, who competed in the two-man event aboard a hired sled, with no home track to train on and a start list dominated by Alpine nations. They finished in 37th place, timed across four heats down the Olympic sliding centre, and carried their flag at the opening ceremony beside athletes from sixty-seven other nations.

This commemorative run set reproduces the official Lillehammer ’94 run card for the Trinidad and Tobago sled in cold-foil-stamped heavyweight stock, with a numbered base plate of brushed aluminium and a maple runner block cut to the sled’s racing profile. Each set is logged under its own number and packed in a canvas sleeve stamped “Lillehammer ’94 — Two-Man — Trinidad and Tobago”. The sled itself, piloted by Gregory Sun with Curtis Harry as brakeman, took its place at the top of the hill on 19 February 1994 and finished the Games in 37th place of the two-man field. Two bobsledders, one nation, one first appearance.

“There is almost real, and almost a god, in that order.”

Paraguay — A Letter to the Editor · The Asunción Ledger · Hancock — Field Error Reference, Revision 1793

One bulletin was pinned at the depot’s lost & found desk on the morning of the start, numbered after the sled’s own run card: LOST — One Country (Kingdom of Norway) · Bulletin No. LF-94/NO-037, which reports the country itself slipping from the sled’s pocket in the third turn and found with a recipe card for the 37th-place syrup. The desk later wrote, from the same bulletin, an apology to the elder of the two Games — To the Summer Olympic Games · A Letter of Apology, on the four years kept since Athens 1896 and the line on the syrup card we had misfiled as a name.
