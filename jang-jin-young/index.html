Jang Jin-young (장진영; 14 June 1972 – 1 September 2009) was a South Korean actress whose screen career, from the runway to the final broadcast, runs across twelve years and a great deal of weather. This boxed edition collects that record as the estate and the catalogue have agreed it: model call sheets and the pageant file from the 1992 Miss Korea contest, the television debut in the drama Angel in My Heart (1997), the film debut in Ghost in Love (1999), the breakthrough that opened the door wide, Sorum (2001), and the seasons that followed.

From the breakthrough onward, the collection follows the range for which Jang was praised — the romantic films Over the Rainbow (2002), Scent of Love (2003), Singles (2003) and Between Love and Hate (2006); the biographical Blue Swallow (2005), in which she played the writer Hwang Sun-young; and the drama series Lobbyist (2007), her last appearance on television. Critical praise, festival entries, and the award record she accumulated along the way are reproduced in the third sleeve. The box is issued in a small numbered edition; nine copies remain at counter 4.
