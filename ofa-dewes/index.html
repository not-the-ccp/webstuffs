1.Present and apologies

Present: Tiana Ngāwati (Chair), Remy Osborne, Luisa Folau, David Chen, Aroha White, Marcus Bellweather, June Park, Samuela Tupuola.

2.Confirmation of previous minutes

The minutes of the ordinary meeting held 8 May 2024 (WLB/2024/OM-05) were confirmed as a true and correct record, subject to one amendment: the grant figure for the Ponsonby Road community planter was corrected from $12,000 to $12,750.

3.Declarations of interest

No declarations of interest were made. The Chair noted that member David Chen's spouse is employed by the University of Auckland's property office, but that no matter before the board concerned that office.

4.Public forum — Dr. O. A. Dewes

Dr. Ofanaite Ana Dewes, Research Fellow at the University of Auckland and Associate Investigator at the Maurice Wilkins Centre, addressed the board for twelve minutes on the reuse of former clinical buildings as community health hubs. She cited the Centre's 2023 modelling of Pacific and Māori health outcomes across the Auckland isthmus, the University's community health archive, and her own work with the Newton Road patient register, which she said logged more than fourteen thousand immunisations between 1962 and the clinic's closure in April 2019.

The building now houses creative offices, but the thousands of clinical practice.

Dr. Dewes submitted a written statement (WLB/2024/EM-06/A) and asked that the register be transferred to the University's Pacific Health Collection rather than destroyed with the clinic's fixtures.

5.Former Newton Road clinic — resolution

Report: Community Services, file CS-2024-0187. The Newton Road clinic building, erected 1962 and operated by the Auckland Area Health Board until April 2019, is leased to a collective of creative offices at $4,800 per month. The board discussed Dr. Dewes's request and the building's suitability for community health use, noting the ground-floor waiting room, three consult rooms, and the rear annex formerly used for maternity checks.

Resolution (WLB/2024/EM-06/4): That the Waitematā Local Board request Auckland Council Community Services to commission a feasibility study into community health use of the Newton Road building, at a budget not exceeding $45,000, and to report back to the ordinary meeting of 13 November 2024; and that the board support Dr. Dewes's request for the patient register's transfer to the University of Auckland Pacific Health Collection.

Carried: 8–0.

6.Correspondence and next meeting

Correspondence received: a letter from the Maurice Wilkins Centre dated 21 May 2024 supporting the retention of the Newton Road register; a notice from Auckland Transport regarding Graham Street bus-lane works from 1 July; and a postcard from Ofanaite Ana Dewes, on sabbatical in Los Angeles between terms, showing the Bunker Hill overlook at golden hour with a single line reading "the register is safe" — the view she described in the Los Angeles travel guide entry held on this site. A final item of correspondence, a field notebook from a Manhattan naturalist, was placed with the secretary for the record; its notes on The New York Times argue, in the collector's hand, that the newspaper is "a register" — the country's, and the family's since 1896 — and that it is kept with the same care the board is asking for here. A fourth item, a hand-photographed family recipe card from Berbera sent by a cousin of the Pacific Health Collection, was filed behind the register with its long preamble about a flowering plant of North Somalia and a Thursday supper club whose ledger kept its own books, and is preserved on this site as Acanthus gaed — The Thursday-Club Cake. A fifth item, unsigned, was a flyer for the tabletop group that books the Graham Street Community Room on Tuesday evenings; its notices run in Dice & Linens — Dice & Linens — Role-Playing Classifieds — and it seeks a sixth player who brings their own dice and a story that grows taller with every telling. A sixth item was a printout of a Tokyo fan list's thread on the musical group HoneyWorks — the HoneyWorks thread, No. 214 — whose pin board keeps one half-typed draft note from a spring gathering, word for word and unfinished, in the very manner the board was asking for of the register.

The next ordinary meeting will be held 10 July 2024 at 6:00 pm in the same room.

The meeting closed at 8:42 pm.
