Error TM-2008 — Publication Ceased

Transmission · London, England · ISSN 1749-561X · Status: not reproducible

Error code

TM-2008

Message

The requested issue is not in stock. Transmission ceased publication in 2008. No further copies will be dispatched, and no back issues are held at this office.

Reported by

Transmission editorial office — 56 Clerkenwell Close, London EC1R 0QL — a non-profit publication written and produced by volunteers.

Incident summary

Transmission was a literary magazine in the United Kingdom, published three times a year from a basement office off Clerkenwell Road. It ran for seventeen issues between spring 2003 and winter 2008, each issue stapled in the office and folded to A5, with a press run of nine hundred copies and a mailing list of four hundred and twelve subscribers. The magazine carried poetry, short fiction, and essays, and paid no one: editors, readers, and the distributor all worked without fee. Issue seventeen, dated December 2008, was the last; the masthead was withdrawn in the same week, and the office was cleared in February 2009.

From the final trustees’ minute, 14 May 2008: The Chair noted that member David Chen's spouse is employed by the letter. The entry stands as recorded; the magazine does not.

Troubleshooting

Can the error be cleared by retrying? No. The magazine is not forthcoming; the printer’s account was settled and the type was distributed.

Where were back issues kept? At the editorial office, 56 Clerkenwell Close, London. The office is closed. Enquiries should go to the British Library, which holds a complete run.

Who produced it? Volunteers. There was no staff, no publisher, and no board after May 2008.
