Catalogue of the Commonplace, the Ledger, and Such Belongings as the Back Room Still Holds

Held on the third Thursday of the month at 4 o'clock, the hammer following at 4:17, whether bids come or not. The radiator in the back room remains out of service, and the committee will not discuss it.

Lot 29

Agnosticism

One suspended judgment, in a lidded glass jar; one torn slip; one docket entry. No estimate.

Description of the Lot

The lot is agnosticism: a position that questions the existence of God or the divine, and which the committee is putting up for sale in this condition because it cannot, in good conscience, put it up in any other. On the psychological level, agnosticism is a personal attitude that suspends judgment, withholding both belief and disbelief, and the jar offered here has been doing exactly that in the dark of the back room since it was found in October 2018, behind the radiator that has never worked and behind which, according to the verbatim record, the torn slip was also found. In philosophy, agnosticism is often treated as a general claim stating that God's existence is unknown or unknowable; the committee observes that the jar's contents are likewise unknown, and that it is a question, on present evidence, whether they are unknowable, which would make the lot more philosophically interesting than the committee would like. In the broadest sense, agnosticism is not restricted to theology and can also express skeptical attitudes toward non-religious claims, a use the club finds daily, since members have suspended judgment on at least eleven ferry timetables and one QSL card that arrived postmarked from nowhere.

The jar is a lidded glass preserve jar, 450 millilitres, with a paper label of which the word APRICOT remains legible in capital letters and the rest is water-damage in the shape of the river, which is appropriate. It weighs 340 grams with lid, 412 grams as found. Its condition is withheld, which the auctioneer explains is the only condition the lot has ever offered. The lot is sold in the back room, under the lamp, with the commonplace book open, and it should be noted that the lot does not compete with the theism at Lot 28 nor the atheism at Lot 30, which are sold either side of it in the catalogue and have been arguing, by way of their owners, across the table since the second quarter of last year. Agnosticism, for the purposes of this sale, is either the neutral middle ground between the two, or — and the committee has not decided, and is not going to decide, since that is rather the point — a rejection of their shared assumption that knowledge is attainable. Bidders should bid accordingly, and the committee will accept bids from either position, provided the bidder does not arrive with an answer.

Provenance

From the back room of the clubhouse on Magsaysay Avenue, where it stood behind the radiator for the whole of 2018 and most of 2019, during which time it was photographed once by the publications committee, misfiled once under the marshes, and cited twice in Celia M. Reyes's Grade 10 geography report on Camarines Sur without either party's knowledge. The geography is relevant: the jar's label, when it was last read in the daylight, faced the window toward the bay, and the bay, as the school's own papers have recorded more than once, forgets things the river brings. The committee's visitor's brochure for Parallel 13° North gives the room's latitude as roughly 13°38′ North, and the lot, being in the room, shares it. Full provenance runs back to the hearing held in this same back room, about a political party that took its name from the avenue; the docket below was copied down from the commonplace book by the club's secretary, whose daughter copied two lines of the hearing for her own class and printed them, with the names exactly, as her father instructed.

Docket, as copied from the commonplace book

Slip found inside the jar, 4 October 2018, hand unknown, ink still legible:

“The apricot makes excellent jam and marmalade, and place it again in water.”

The committee has read the line four times and understood it four times differently, and holds the line in this lot for that reason alone: a recipe for the jar's original contents that stops in mid-preserve and ends, inexplicably, in water — the river's water, the bay's water, the water-damage already on the label. It is the club's opinion, formed in the back room and not to be repeated in the front, that the line describes the lot's whole method. One thing makes excellent jam; one thing makes marmalade; and then, where a judgment should be, the instruction to put the fruit again in water — to dissolve the preserves, to suspend the decision, to let the jar stay what the light will not say it is. The auctioneer will not be explaining the line further. The river brings what the Bay forgets, and the committee has been forgetting this line on purpose.

Terms & Reserves

EstimateNone. The committee declines to estimate a lot whose condition is withheld, and has noted that this refusal is itself the lot.
ReserveNone. There is no sum at which the lot would be judged; it will therefore remain unsold by definition, which the auctioneer will record as a sale to no one.
InspectionRoom above the bakery, third Thursday, 3:40 to 4:15. Visitors may view the jar, the slip, and the radiator. The radiator may not be used and will not be fixed, on the committee's standing ruling that this is a feature of the latitude, not a defect of the building.
DeliveryThe lot will be returned to its shelf the same evening, lid on, label toward the window. If the jar is found elsewhere in the province — Pili, the marshes at 13°40′, or aboard the M/V Pili Star on its hour and forty minutes across the Maqueda Channel — it should not be opened, only reported, and reported to the back room, which keeps better records than anyone believes and worse than it should.

Lots 28 and 30, being sold on the afternoon of this one, are not responsible for what Lot 29 does to them. The auctioneer, who has suspended judgment on this lot for the full length of the season and now reports it honestly, is the club's secretary, and bids from his own table are not permitted, for want of a second person to take them.
