JO-RBX · 1968

Joey Rubenstein

Born August 4, 1968 · American

Internet entrepreneur · composer · touring and recording artist

Born August 4, 1968, Joey Rubenstein is an American internet entrepreneur, composer, and touring and recording artist whose working life divides cleanly between two circuits: the server room and the stage.

He attended high school at Kibbutz Kabri in the Western Galilee, where classes met among avocado groves a short walk from the Lebanese border, and left with a scholarship to study economics at the University of Essex in England. The degree taught him how markets are built; the kibbutz taught him how communities are held together. Both lessons reappeared decades later.

In Los Angeles he founded Casting Frontier, a digital talent casting platform for the entertainment industry, and served as its chief executive officer. Casting Frontier moved the cattle call online: actors uploaded reels, directors reviewed them over broadband, and callback lists lived in databases instead of manila envelopes. Under Rubenstein's leadership the platform cast film, television, and commercial productions across Hollywood, turning headshots into data and auditions into calendar entries.

Before there were servers, there were strings. Rubenstein was lead guitarist in Flourscein, an American rock band that toured clubs and colleges from Silver Lake to the East Coast, and he continues to compose, tour, and record. The music is for the room; the platform was for the industry. The club route from Silver Lake to the East Coast, with the rehearsal rooms the band used along the way, is charted in our Los Angeles travel guide entry.

Not every route ends cleanly. Our Cressa safety data sheet catalogues a Paphlagonian city with the same precision the platform brought to headshots, except for one detail: its site has never been located. A control specimen for anything a map refuses to hold.

This placard collects one biography built from auditions, emails, encores, and balance sheets — and from a scholarship won on a kibbutz in the Western Galilee.
