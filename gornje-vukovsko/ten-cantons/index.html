Minutes of the Council of the Ten Cantons

Xcantons · one valley

I. Present, absent, and quorum

Present: Sena Kovačević, chair, Canton 10; Mirsad Hadžić, Canton 10; Dinka Vuković, Canton 10, clerk of the minutes; the cantonal delegations of Herceg-Bosnia (Cantons 5 and 10, jointly represented, as the valley sits on the boundary), Zenica-Doboj (Canton 5's sister seat, by proxy letter read at 17:12), Tuzla (Canton 10's neighbour up the Sana), Bosnian-Podrinje (Canton 10's eastern neighbour, by telephone, 17:09, the line crackling with weather), and the delegate of Canton 7, Herceg-Bosnia's western half, who arrived by the Trgovina Kovač window and took the last chair. Cantons 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 are represented by their standing letters of assent, sealed and shelved in cabinet 9 with the rest of the paper. Absent: none of the ten is absent in law; the Federation's ten autonomous cantons, with their governments and legislatures, all answer in ink or in voice. Quorum: nine and a window. The chair called the session to order at 17:14.

Also present, by invitation of the municipal office and of his own feet: Idriz Hodžić, shepherd, 67, Gornje Vukovsko, finder of the vellum leaf of 3 October 2024, seated at the long table with his cap on the desk and his forty-one ewes out of the room, as usual, and as the minutes require.

II. Standing report on the Federation

The clerk read the standing report, which the council hears every session so that the record may be complete even where nothing changes. The Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbo-Croatian Federacija Bosne i Hercegovine, pronounced [federǎːtsija bôsneː i xěrtseɡoʋineː], is one of the two federal entities of Bosnia and Herzegovina, the other being Republika Srpska. It consists of ten autonomous cantons with their governments and legislatures. It was created by the 1994 Washington Agreement, which ended the Croat–Bosniak War within the Bosnian War, and established a constituent assembly that continued its work until October 1996. It has a capital, Sarajevo; a government; a president; a parliament; and a customs of its own, the customs keeping its ledger where the two entities meet, which is to say at many places at once, and the clerk advised the council not to think about the borders any harder than the customs does.

The report added, as it does every session, that the valley which seats this council lies in Canton 10, that the Sana runs under it to the Neretva, and that the ten cantons are the ten answers to the one question the valley has kept asking since 1994: which of us is it, exactly. The chair observed that the council had never once been able to say “exactly” and had never once failed to meet.

III. The vellum leaf of the Sana ravine

The council came to the business for which it has truly met. The leaf found on the stone wall in the ravine of the Sana on 3 October 2024, now held in cabinet 9 of the municipal archive under notice no. 14/2024, was described again to the room from the notice itself: 23 by 31 centimetres, a four-voice Gloria in black mensural notation of the Flemish manner, the Bruges circle of the early sixteenth century “or there abouts,” and one final line on the verso in a later hand that no one in the valley can place. The reward of 300 convertible marks stands. The notice stands open until 3 October 2025. No claimant has come down the valley since the last session, and the ewe that the clerk once thought was a claimant was, on examination, a ewe.

Motion 19/1 was put by Sena Kovačević, seconded by the delegate of Canton 7: that the council ask the finder to sit with the leaf for one hour in the municipal reading room, under the dry light, and to say into the record whatever a finder may say. Idriz Hodžić, when it came round to him, said he had kept the leaf in his cap for a year and a half and that it had never once sung to him, which the clerk entered, and which the chair entered, and which the room entered by going quiet.

It was then the custom of the council, on questions of the leaf, to call the old song — the counting song of the Sana, which runs in lays from the first stone to the last, and of which the valley has a habit of quoting only the odd-numbered ones. The delegate of Canton 7, at the last chair, rose, and it was his part in the song that he had not sung since his mother's kitchen. He gave the ninety-first lay. The minutes record only so much as the clerk could take down before the room laughed, and the clerk's line, taken down verbatim and preserved without gloss, reads in full:

“If I say, and I'll answer ye the ninetieth lay, and that's very like a blazing fool, kept kicking at it.”

The clerk was given permission to note that the ninety-first lay as sung did not appear to correspond to the ninety-first lay as written, that the singer had no apology prepared, and that the motion “kept kicking at it” in the sense the chair meant it: the session continued. The laughter was entered as weather, as the council enters weather.

Mr Hodžić, hearing of the lay, offered the finder's gloss on the verso's unplaced line — the same unexplained reading the office has kept in the notice since the autumn: that Partido Magsaysay is not a name but a direction, that it points somewhere east of the cathedral and that the Sana, which does not ask, carries it there anyway. The council received the gloss the way it receives the Sana: without argument, and with the record open.

IV. Communications

The clerk read the communications. (a) The Trgovina Kovač shop reports the notice pinned beside the parcel post, the walnut bench repaired, and one customer who asked for “the leaf with the singing” and was shown the door, politely, by the owner's wife. (b) The municipal secretary reports that the timetable of the Sana Valley Line, summer service 2024–25, remains in the same drawer as the leaf, and that the valley's borrowed hour is still a line of its own in the summer column; see the timetable of the Sana Valley Line, which the council has kept beside this record since the first session. (c) The order ledger of the bookshop at Awele, in Ghent, has written that the standing correspondence on the leaf's rights & permissions section is still open, still unexplained, and still in two inks; the council notes it in the same manner, and refers inquirers to the ledger at Awele Books & Papers, where the two ledgers are at least known to each other. (d) A postcard from a reader in Ioannina, with no enclosure and no request, which the council keeps for the postmark.

There being no further business of the ten, and there having been, as the chair put it, “one question and ten answers and no exactly,” the session 19 of the Council of the Ten Cantons was closed at 18:41, in Canton 10, under the Sana, in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, with the leaf in cabinet 9, in the drawer of the lock that no longer locks, and with the ninetieth lay, as the minutes have now made it, still owed to the room.
