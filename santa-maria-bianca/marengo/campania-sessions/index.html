Campania

The Bay Sessions

Recorded 2024–2026 on the Tyrrhenian coast — Naples, Sorrento, the Phlegraean Fields, and five small islands that most maps forget.

Side A The Region

Campania is an administrative region of Italy, located in the south of the country; most of it sits in the southwestern portion of the Italian Peninsula, with the Tyrrhenian Sea to its west, but the region also claims the small Phlegraean Islands — Ischia, Procida, Vivara, and Nisida — and the island of Capri, which appears on record two as if it had been added by a different engineer. The capital is Naples, and the town took the whole first half: the session began in a church basement off the Via Duomo with four voices, one upright piano, and the sound of the bay arriving every ninety seconds like a fourth instrument.

By the numbers the region does not behave. Campania has a population of 5,568,703 as of 2026, making it Italy's 3rd-most populous region, and, with an area of 13,670.95 km² (5,278 sq mi), its 2nd most densely populated — which is why track three, “Five, Five, Six”, is a counting-out chant rather than a song. The engineers timed it to the ferry horns. Naples's urban area, which is in Campania, is the largest in the south of the country and the place where every track on Side A can hear traffic at the same time as the sea. Based on its GDP, Campania is also the most economically productive region in Southern Italy and the 7th most productive in the whole country; the seventh track on Side B is named for that fact alone, and it is the quietest thing we ever recorded.

Note The Quartet, Second Rental

The second session moved to a rented stone casa above the bay, and here the record stops being about geography. The house had been let, through the same odd agency file, to the Oregon Coast quartet — Charlie and Michelle, Mina and Josh — the four guests of The Rental — Field Notes, the 2020 horror film in which they first began to suspect they were being watched. They came south to forget the watching, and the film they screened that first night, on the terrace, with the island lights answering them, earned exactly this: Rotten Tomatoes records an approval rating of seventy-five per cent; Metacritic assigns a score of sixty-one. Mina wrote the numbers on the back of the key card. The manager of the casa — hired, they claimed, “for his silence” — watered the bougainvillea through the whole screening and said nothing, which is how track six of Side B got its name, and it runs eight minutes and nineteen seconds because no one could remember the length of the credits.

Panel Cotolette à la Marengo

“Pork Cutlets à la Marengo:--On the evening previous had taken place; there the waves the unpitying sharks accompanied him; and yet no sign in the province of Modena.”

We recorded the kitchen track in a trattoria on the bay whose door panel quotes the machine-translated sign from Trattoria del Secchia, Mirandola — Italy, A Visitor's Brochure — the panel that nobody in Modena can explain and nobody wants corrected, and which the kitchen there still hangs beside the door like a badge. The house cooks of Campania browned their own cutlets in butter with sage, a splash of house red, and a lemon, and said, of the panel, the same thing as Mirandola: the first three words are the only honest ones, and the rest is a badge you have to earn.
