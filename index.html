Webstuffs

A miscellany of guides, records, recipes, notices, letters and other things worth keeping. Some of it is useful. Some of it is true. A few pages are both.

Start anywhere. Most pages lead somewhere else.

For a dramatised history of the governing body founded in 1886, see the Tennis New Zealand radio play excerpt.

If you prefer a quiet stroll through historic Northwest Portland, the Bates–Seller House walking tour traces a lumber merchant's legacy from the streetcar lines to a copper whale weather vane.

For a slower read, there is a forum thread about Pedro do Rosário, a municipality on the Maranhão coast where the bumba-meu-boi drums come out every June for the feast of São Pedro.

For the release-week timetable of a worship band, the We Are Fearless departure board follows the long journey from rehearsal to the top of the charts.

For a hand-tied weapon for the beach, the Saltfleet Whip is a twelve-foot leather lash finished on the shingle of a Lincolnshire coastal village.

For a schoolgirl's report on a 1914 Keystone comedy where Charlie Chaplin staggers drunk through a domestic disaster, the Mabel's Married Life film history project reproduces a term paper complete with master's marginalia.
