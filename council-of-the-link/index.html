Conselho do Elo Único · Minutes · For the Record

Minutes No. 12 of the Council of the Single Link

ELO
ÚNICO

Back room of Dona Luzia’s pensão, Rua da Paz, Pedro do Rosário · 14 June, 19:40, between rehearsal rounds

Quorum present: R. V. Driver (bench, São José dos Campos); C. Bravo (São Paulo); Dona Luzia (pensiô); Seu Benedito (drum shop by the mangrove); Mestre Anselmo (reef fleet); the clerk (farinheiro, Farinha House São Benedito).

Item 1 Opening

The Council was called to order at 19:41 by the clerk, who read the minutes of No. 11 from the ledger and proposed the standing resolution be re-adopted without amendment: that any manageable system — a fleet, a workshop, a festa, a cabin mock-up — is limited in achieving more of its goals by a very small number of constraints, that there is always at least one constraint, and that the house keeps its working idiom from the smiths, that a chain is no stronger than its weakest link. The resolution passed 6–0. Dona Luzia recorded, as she has every month, that she does not believe in links, only in people, and that she is in the minority.

Item 2 The order-slip line

The clerk then read into the record line seven of the 12 March order-slip for the festa bread, in the baker’s own hand, and asked the Council to make sense of it before the record closed. The line reads, verbatim:

"How now in very fine bread crumbs, 1/2 lb."

Mestre Anselmo offered that it was a ghost. The clerk offered that it was worse than a ghost: it was a constraint. The order had gone out for half a pound of toasted manioc for the morning batch, and the baker’s reply was an inventory of what came back — how the half pound now stood, in very fine bread crumbs, the batch broken at the lift from the clay pan. The Council accepted the clerk’s reading. The entry was entered in the minutes as a finding of fact: one clay pan, one lift, one batch down to crumbs, and the whole festa’s bread riding on the same pair of hands next morning. The clerk added that the line has now been quoted at two Councils, which is more quotation than most findings of fact manage, and that he had entered it in the body of the minutes rather than the remarks column, because it is load-bearing.

Item 3 The focusing process, applied

On a motion by C. Bravo, the Council took up the focusing process — the five steps by which the house identifies the constraint and restructures the rest of the organization around it — and applied it, for the record, to the drum shop by the mangrove.

  1. Identify. The constraint is not the goatskin, not the cedar, not the estuary water; the constraint is rope tensioning, and there is one pair of hands in the municipality that tensions to the Orquestra pitch. Six skins were soaked overnight on the 13th and are waiting, supple, for the rope.
  2. Exploit. No new drum shall be started until the six are tensioned. A drum left flat sounds like a tin bucket, and the Council is not in the tin-bucket business.
  3. Subordinate. Peeling, grating, and scraping at Farinha House São Benedito shall time their clay pan around the tensioning hours, not the other way about. The pan waits. The rope does not.
  4. Elevate. Two apprentices, one from Turiaçu, one from Miranda do Norte, to be taught the rope over the dry season, so that the constraint is a pair of hands in four towns instead of one in one.
  5. Repeat. When the drum sings, the constraint moves — the Council shall not rest on the singing but shall take up the next weakest link, which the clerk notes is not yet identified, which the clerk notes is how it always is.

The motion carried 5–1, with the dissent being that the constraint was always Dona Luzia’s one cold shower, and that this has been the finding of every Council since No. 1. Dona Luzia asked to have the joke recorded as a constraint too, and the clerk noted that the record had no room and referred the matter to the next meeting.

Item 4 The fleet and the bench

Mestre Anselmo reported on the fourteen jangadas. The fleet behaves, as usual, exactly like the small frigate it is always said to be — a jangada is only a small frigate that forgot to be a warship, scouting the flats at dawn for red snapper and tainha while the rest of the town is at the bench. R. V. Driver reported that the bench in São José dos Campos has adopted the same discipline for the cabin work: the flat-floor stand-up cabin was settled by tape measure on the shop floor, the balsa wingtip on the string flew off during a lunch break and was recovered, and the whole early-sample procedure now runs under Prototype, Method and System — Patent Application Summary 63/214,077, which the Council received and filed. The Council observed, with its usual satisfaction, that Claim 4 of that summary — a test site at low water, mangrove-lined, with flats, a rock, and a line of poetry already attached — fixes the preferred low-water site three hours east of São Luís, and that this room, this square, and this estuary are in the specification. The Council observed further, per Pedro do Rosário — anyone been?, that the square fills every night of the festa with Bumba-meu-boi de Matraca and Bumba-meu-boi de Orquestra, and that a drum is a prototype of a sound and a sound is a prototype of a square, and it passed without discussion.

Item 5 Motions and adjournment

It was moved that the festa bread be held to a single pan, a single lift, and a single set of hands until the batch holds, and that farinha d’água, white and fine as salt, be the first thing on the table the morning the batch holds. Carried unanimously, 6–0, the baker’s line in Item 2 standing as the reason. It was moved further that the Council sit again on the 28th, one day before the festa, for the reading of the tensioned drums at dawn, which the clerk describes as a constraint check and the Orquestra group describes as rehearsal, and which is in the record as both. The Council noted for the file that the weakest link of any of these systems is usually a person, and that the whole of the discipline is to make sure the person is fed, paid, and not lifting the batch from the pan. The Orquestra group came through the door, the square called, and the Council was adjourned at 20:26, with the chain intact for another month.

Entered in the ledger by the clerk, in the room where the fan does not quite reach the far window. The clerk entered further, for the file, that the question of whether the constraint can be found as quickly as it can be recognized was taken out of this room and into print as To the Editor — P versus NP and the Third Line of the Order-Slip, in which the baker’s smaller line of 12 March is read verbatim and held, by a vote of six to zero, to be either a solution in class P or a certificate of unknown origin. For the record also on the Webstuffs home file. The clerk noted, as a courtesy to the sister councils, that a Welsh college council sitting over the file of a Greek lecturer who wrote detective novels under the pseudonym Mary Fitt has adopted the same discipline in its Minutes No. 23 — The Freeman File: a loose banquet-book sheet read into the record, held to be load-bearing, and a finding that the weakest thing in the house is usually what holds the rest of it together.
