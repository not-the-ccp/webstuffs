Walt Disney World Resort

An entertainment and vacation region about 20 miles (32 km) southwest of Orlando, Florida, United States, opened on 1 October 1971 and operated by Disney Experiences, a division of the Walt Disney Company. The manual covers approximately 27,000 acres (42 sq mi; 109 km2): four theme parks, two water parks, four golf courses, conference centers, a competitive sports complex, a major shopping, dining and entertainment complex, and thirty-one Disney-owned resort hotels.

1 · The Parks

Magic Kingdom, the opening park, entered service on 1 October 1971. Cinderella Castle marks the hub, with Adventureland, Frontierland, Liberty Square, Fantasyland, and Tomorrowland laid out around it. A service tunnel network — the utilidors — runs beneath the park, so that cast members and deliveries cross the map without stepping onto a player's stage.

Epcot opened on 1 October 1982, centered on Spaceship Earth and the Future World pavilions. Disney's Hollywood Studios followed on 1 May 1989, opening as Disney-MGM Studios, and Disney's Animal Kingdom on 22 April 1998. The two water parks, Typhoon Lagoon and Blizzard Beach, complete the seven-zone roster.

2 · Encounter: Captain EO

At Epcot's Future World, the Magic Eye Theater ran Captain EO, a 3D presentation directed by Francis Ford Coppola and starring Michael Jackson, from 18 September 1986. The same American singer and songwriter appears as the artist of Stranger in Moscow, Entry SM-1995/05 of the Encyclopaedia of Recorded Song; the theater marquee kept his name for eight years, and the engagement returned, briefly, in 2010. Treat as a recurring encounter: the lights dim, the figure on screen is both a stranger and a star, and the room holds that state without resolving it.

3 · Travel and Landings

The monorail runs three service lines — Express, Resort, and Epcot — across a beam loop that joins the Transportation and Ticket Center, the Magic Kingdom, and the resort hotels of the lagoon shore. Three ferries cross the Seven Seas Lagoon from the Transportation and Ticket Center to the Magic Kingdom landing: the Admiral Joe Fowler, the Richard F. Irvine, and the Kingdom Queen. The dockmaster's copy of the opening-day procedures closes with a single line, kept word for word in the margin:

“And had you strolled forward nigh the wharf.”

4 · Field Cache

Lost and found at the Contemporary Resort holds a folder of six prints, unattributed, showing six isolated figures — a figure on a rooftop in snow, a figure at a window through which the street passes without stopping — each lit by the same weather, none of them touching. The photographer's name is not in the log. The entry is kept as found.

5 · The Resort Ledger

A pocket watch turned in at Magic Kingdom guest relations on 14 October 1993 carried a note in a familiar hand: “The watch is a complete, and sitting fast.” The phrase matches a statement first set down verbatim on Kenwood Drive, in Louisville, and has been logged in the resort ledger under lost property, unclaimed, with the annotation kept word for word.
