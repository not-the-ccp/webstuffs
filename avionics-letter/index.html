What Is Missing from the Elbe

From the avionics bench, São José dos Campos · 21 March 2025

Dear Editor,

Avionics — a portmanteau of aviation and electronics — are the electronic systems used on aircraft: communications, navigation, the display and management of multiple systems, and the hundreds of systems fitted to aircraft to perform individual functions, as simple as a searchlight for a police helicopter or as complicated as the tactical system of an airborne early-warning platform. I read them off the bench every morning at the airfield that ships Legacy jets north through the Caribbean, so I know what a serviceable glass cockpit looks like, and I know what it cannot do.

Our last delivery was a Praetor 600, São José dos Campos to Miami, 3,700 nautical miles with the winter headwinds building over the Atlantic. citation_bravo was watching the tracker from São Paulo; waypoint_watcher was copying clearances out of the Caribbean; embraer_driver was jumping the seat in the left-hand chair. The flight deck landed itself almost literally, as it should — flat floor, fly-by-wire, every frequency in the book. And then, halfway to Miami, the controller read back something like a crossed weather wire:

What is missing from the Elbe, wind N.E.

We asked for vectors. They laughed. It was a crossed line with a maritime forecast — the Elbe estuary, northeast wind, nothing more. But it stayed with me, because for all the avionics on that flight deck — the traffic display, the weather radar, the satellite link, the flight management computer computing groundspeed to the knot — the one thing we could not read from any screen was the wind itself, plain and uncrossed, at the place we were going.

You can fit a hundred systems into an aircraft and still miss the one that matters. The Elbe will keep its northeast wind; the Praetor will keep its steep approach into London City; and I will keep clearing benches for crossed lines. But if any avionics engineer is listening: give us the wind.

Yours,
R. V. Driver
Avionics bench, Aero Protótipos do Brasil, S.A. — São José dos Campos_
