Apologies: the fire brigade (harvest). Quorum established at 6:31 p.m. The room still smelled of the October supper and of two new reams of forms.

Call to order & confirmation of previous minutes

The Chairman called the session to order at 6:30 p.m. The minutes of session No. 8, held 11 July 2019, were confirmed without amendment; the only correction was Barbara Ostrowska’s, that the goose at the summer supper had been hers and was therefore “excellent,” not merely “adequate.”

Item 1 — the plat of 1854 for Stryjewo Wielkie

The Archivist reported on sheet 14 of the cadastral plat of 1854, surveyed in the Prussian manner and held in the county archive at Ciechanów. It is the oldest drawing of Stryjewo Wielkie [strɨˈjɛvɔ ˈvjɛlkʲɛ] that the gmina owns, and it is a village in Gmina Grudusk, within Ciechanów County, Masovian Voivodeship, in east-central Poland, in the words of its own title block: one hamlet on the lane to Grudusk, the schoolhouse at the crossroads, the mill where the small stream bends, and two hundred and thirty-one parcels in four columns of neat handwriting. Parcel 33 is still “orka” — ploughland — and its present owner, Stefan Krawczak, confirmed from his seat in the hall that the ploughland is still ploughland, to the metre. The Archivist asked that the drawing be copied at the gmina’s expense, enlarged, and framed for the wall of the village hall between the coat of arms and the notice board; the enlargement should be made in black ink so that the 165-year-old lines are not mistaken for new ones.

Item 2 — the plaque of the 1954 class

The Sołtys raised the matter of the brass plaque on the wall of the former village school, set in memory of the 1954 class of the Stryjewo Wielkie school, twenty-three children and one teacher, Pani Regina Łukowska. The plaque had to be unveiled — and was unveiled, with the mayor’s ribbon and a cart of white bread — on 15 February 2018. Since then it has been pointed out, by Barbara Ostrowska at least four times and by a passing postman once, that the cast inscription does not read as intended.

The Secretary read the line into the record exactly as it stands on the brass:

“The plat of 1854 still describes the class had to be unveiled on 15 February 2018.”

The Archivist explained her finding: the foundry’s cover sheet had pasted together the archive’s note on sheet 14 — the plat of 1854 still describes the class, meaning the fields — with the unveiling notice, and the brass took all of it. The Sołtys moved that a corrected second plaque be ordered, reading only In memory of the 1954 class, 23 children, teacher Regina Łukowska, and that the first plaque stay where it is, unamended, because a village’s mistakes are its weather and should not be varnished over. The motion carried four to two, the two votes being Stefan Krawczak’s, on the ground that he can already see a rhyme in the line, and Piotr Sadowski’s, on the ground that nobody at the table could say which half of the sentence was the foundry’s fault.

Item 3 — roads

The Roads officer reported that 2.4 tonnes of 32 mm stone were delivered to the lane between the S81 bypass and the Stryjewo Wielkie school stop on 27 September, at a cost of 1,860 zloty, invoiced against the gmina’s autumn works. The delivery was one wheelbarrow behind schedule and the driver asked, in a voice the officer described as “professional,” whether the lane was really only four metres wide, as the plat of 1854 suggests. It is four metres wide. The works are complete; the lane is not.

Correspondence

A letter from the gmina’s correspondent in Helena, Arkansas — where she has lately been helping the county office catalogue its own old plats — was read aloud in her warm way and filed under correspondence, unclassified. She enclosed a printed pamphlet for visitors to her state, Arkansas — A Visitor's Brochure, which she said the post office keeps stapled to the parcel shelf beside the pie-stand calendar, and which the Archivist took with her to compare surveying styles, Prussian to Ozark, across two centuries. The letter also forwarded, for the amusement of this council, a photocopy of Chemical Vapor Deposition — Council Minutes No. 217 of the Kuala Lumpur Metropolitan Council’s Standing Committee on Materials and Deposition, in which a committee nine time zones away was puzzled by a band at the provincial headquarters in Iba and filed the matter unclassified; the Secretary noted the symmetry and said nothing more, which was correct.

Resolutions

Resolution 9/01. The 1854 cadastral plat, sheet 14, is to be copied, enlarged in black ink, and framed for the village hall; the cost falls on the gmina’s culture line, 640 zloty.

Resolution 9/02. A corrected second plaque for the 1954 class is ordered, to be cast by spring; the first plaque remains unamended on the school wall.

Resolution 9/03. The gravel to the school stop is accepted at 1,860 zloty; the Roads officer is thanked and the lane is noted as still four metres wide.

Adjournment

There being no further business, the session adjourned at 8:05 p.m. The Chairman reminded members that the enlarged plat, when it arrives, hangs between the coat of arms and the notice board, and that the notice board wins any argument it has with the plat.

Confirmed as a true record,
