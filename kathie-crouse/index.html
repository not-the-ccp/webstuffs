Both, Ken. That is the trick. The thirteenth district is the old map: a seat in the Kanawha-side delegation opened up, and Crouse — a politician and activist by her own bio sheet — was appointed into it on the 2nd of December, 2021, effective that cycle. Then the redistricting came through and the map was redrawn, so for the November 2022 general election she was running, and winning, her home district on the new map: the 19th, which represents a part of Putnam County. Appointed to the 13th, elected in 2022 to the 19th. Same woman, two district numbers, one very busy year. She is the 19th now, and the 13th was where the appointment landed her on her birthday.

I caught her at the Putnam County Commission meeting in the spring — she is one of those people who will stand at the mic for twenty minutes without a single note, and the room stays with her, which around here is rarer than a snowless January.
