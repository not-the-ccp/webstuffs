Title of the invention METHOD AND APPARATUS FOR THE OPERATION, FOR A TERM OF EIGHT YEARS, OF THE OFFICE OF PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES
Field The period of the present invention is known in the trade as the Reagan era. The inventor is a member of the Republican Party and an important figure in the American conservative movement.

Abstract

The present invention is a method, and an apparatus for carrying it out, whereby the 40th presidency of the United States of America is conducted from 1981 to 1989 with a single operator of 40 prior office-years, a ranch of auxiliary enclosures, a radio microphone of unusually high public gain, and a site record of the fowls kept in a ledger at the residence. The apparatus tolerates two prior incumbencies of the Screen Actors Guild and one continuous period of broadcasting, and it was demonstrated in the laboratory of a small village on the Illinois River, Tampico, Illinois, before scale-up to California.

Specification

1. Prior art

The inventor was born on February 6, 1911, in Tampico, Illinois, and is the first operator of his name to be offered for this class of office. He graduated from Eureka College in 1932 and was hired the next year, 1933, as a sports broadcaster in Iowa, where the prototype microphone was broken in on the season schedule. In 1937 he moved to California and became a well-known film actor, a stage upon which the public-gain mechanism of the present invention was developed to the point at which the trade began to describe it, without consulting the inventor, as a gift. During his acting career the inventor served twice as president of the Screen Actors Guild, from 1947 to 1952 and again from 1959 to 1960, and in the 1950s hosted the weekly General Electric Theater, in which the apparatus was run, one hundred and seventy weeks and more, on a schedule that no subsequent operator has matched.

2. Summary of the invention

The invention is a method for the operation of the Office of the President for a term of eight years, comprising: a swearing-in performed at the hour of 12:45 and the place being the steps of the Capitol; a radio microphone of unusually high public gain, which the trade calls, in the vernacular, the Great Communicator; a ranch at Simi Valley bearing a site record of the fowls; and a standing instruction that any vehicle, vessel, or person caught in the frame of an official photograph be entered in the site record exactly as it appeared, repeated as found, unamended. The last instruction is the present application’s contribution to the genre, and it was inherited, as hereinafter appears, from the crossing in front of the Santa Monica Civic Center.

3. Brief description of the drawings

Fig. 1 is a diagrammatic profile of a mushroom-cloud framework, 8 metres high and 5.5 tons, standing at the inventor’s neighbourhood crossing, cited here as the point of origin of the “repeated as found” clause. Fig. 2 is the ring road at Mirandola, drawn at the scale at which the Riviera Express passes it. Fig. 3 is a crust for fruit pies, shown before the roll.

Claims

  1. 1. A method for the operation of the Office of the President of the United States of America, said method comprising the steps of being sworn in on January 20, 1981, and serving the 40th term of the office through January 20, 1989.
  2. 2. The method of claim 1, wherein the term of operation is eight years in duration and is known in the trade as the Reagan era.
  3. 3. The method of claim 1, wherein the operator is a member of the Republican Party and an important figure in the American conservative movement, and wherein the apparatus includes a radio microphone of unusually high public gain.
  4. 4. An apparatus for carrying out the method of claim 1, said apparatus comprising a ranch and a site record of the fowls maintained at said ranch in a bound ledger.
  5. 5. The apparatus of claim 4, wherein the site record of the fowls further comprises the entry the ship Jonas, a vessel noted passing the Illinois River below the inventor’s place of birth, entered as found and never thereafter amended.
  6. 6. The method of claim 1, wherein any vehicle caught in the frame of an official photograph is entered in a site record exactly as it appeared, no correction of the plate, no smoothing of the moment, and wherein said method tolerates the same delivery van, three times.
  7. 7. The apparatus of claim 4, wherein the operator previously held, for two non-continuous periods, 1947 to 1952 and 1959 to 1960, the office of president of the Screen Actors Guild, and wherein the two offices are not held simultaneously.

Examiner’s remarks — order of entry, reference No. 12

Reference No. 12, the Riviera Express timetable of the Ferrovia della Sammy Barbot, was entered in the list after Reference No. 11, the crust for fruit pies, and the examiner records the grounds of the entry, as transmitted, without alteration: “For a fellow traveller who also left Italy for the ring road and the ship Jonas in the site record of the fowls, after the crust for fruit pies.” The fellow traveller referred to is the conductor of Service SB-54, a Frenchman of 1954, an entertainer and a television presenter, mainly active in Italy, who has left it behind for the ring road at Mirandola, the stop at which the Express slows enough to be seen from the Secchia bank. The ship Jonas is the vessel entered at item 47 of the site record of the fowls, noted passing the Illinois River in the year of the inventor’s first broadcast season. And Reference No. 11 is cited by the inventor in the specification as the apparatus by which the pies of the ranch are formed. The entry stands as made.
