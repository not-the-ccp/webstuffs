Goygol Plain Gazette

Classified Column · No. 118 · Wednesday 12 November 2025 · price 2 manats, 3 in the frost

Pinned to the south shed wall, Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00–18:00, and whenever. The ads desk takes milk, fleece, hens and grammar in that order.

From the ads desk

The classified column is kept on a board nailed to the south shed wall, to the right of the hens’ own coop, where the notice with the grammar of its own still hangs. Every ad below is numbered against this issue and priced in manats, the plain’s own money. Milk heads the column, as milk always does: it is the white liquid food (though it comes in shades of yellow, cream, pink and, once, brown) produced by the mammary glands of lactating mammals, and on this plain it is collected from the farm animals as an agricultural product the way other places collect wheat — by hand, at dawn, in a bucket, with the ledger open. The herd this issue is drawing on is the record’s own: 63 Qarabagh angora goats, 41 Akkaraman ewes and 112 free-range hens, kept under the shared gate whose whole history fills Case No. 44-41 of 2025 at the Goygol District Court. Read the column in order. The desk is not responsible for what the grammar does.

For sale Dairy

118-M1

Milk, by the litre.

A white liquid food, produced the plain’s way by the mammary glands of lactating mammals: 63 angora goats, 41 ewes, one gate. 2.10 manats the litre; the cream line, 3.50. Morning yield per the ledger of 14 March 2025: 9.4 litres at dawn, 0.0 at the evening — the ledger kept the milk; the bucket did not (see 118-L1). In July, with the clover in, the milk may come cream, yellow or faintly pink; brown is rarer and will be announced separately. — N. Hasanova, tenant of the south shed.

118-M2

Colostrum, the first milk.

Fourteen manats the hundred, while it lasts. It comes thick and yellow, and it is not the rest of the season in any useful sense: the first milk carries antibodies and immune-modulating components that strengthen the young animal’s immune system against many diseases, and the immune factors run through the whole run afterwards — milk immunity, the station calls it. It is the primary source of nutrition for young mammals before they are able to digest solid food, and the station sells it on that ground, to that customer, at that price. The Baku mill is not a customer. — The Goygol station.

118-M3

Composition, for the curious.

Calcium and protein by the glass, and — the plain being the plain — lactose and saturated fat on the side, both of them, in the same glass, no separate charge. The enzyme lactase is needed to break down the lactose; see 118-W1 for a tutor. No one on the plain disputes the milk’s ledger. — The Goygol station.

Lost & found The gate, the bucket, the reflection

118-L1

Lost: one bucket, at 03:20.

One bucket, last seen at the south shed gate at 03:20 on 14 March 2025, together with the morning’s 9.4 litres. The entry in the ledger reads door, 03:20, frost; the hens were the reason and the frost was the consequence, per the record. The bucket was thought to have reappeared in the court yard on the morning of the weighing, 8 November 2025, when Exhibit C — one fleece, 2.71 kilograms — went over the scale and the defendant’s own bucket stood where the light fell on it. The owner was asked what the reflection referred to. Her answer, taken down as spoken: “Well, it’s not my reflection in the court yard — payable to the side, and the remainder of the south shed of the fact.” The desk files it as written: the fine is in fact payable to the side — to the Goygol station — and the grammar, like the notice on the door, is its own. Reward one litre, at 2.10, not the cream line. — The ads desk, on information.

118-F1

Found: a gram of frost, still in the ledger.

One gram of frost, or its entry, found in the ledger of 14 March 2025. Believed to be the property of the angora lot, which took it through the open gate at 03:20 and paid for it in the fleece. Will not be returned; it has been weighed, docketed and enforced. — The Agricultural Division.

Wanted Tutors and padlocks

118-W1

Wanted: a tutor in lactase.

One person, man or woman, to teach this plain the enzyme lactase, which is needed to break down lactose — a deficiency of the plain, not of the milk. Evenings, after the 18:00 the notice grants; pay per glass, four glasses the limit per student. Hens may not be presented as students; the coop is closed and has been since the docket note of 8 November. The student who first breaks down a glass without a ledger entry will be entered in the station diary. — Elchin Veliyev, keeper of the herd.

118-W2

Wanted: one padlock, and the key split.

Per the order in Case No. 44-41 of 2025: the gate is to be shared on a padlock, the key split, and the hens kept in their own coop until the frost is off the plain. The padlock was hung at 07:45; the split, it appears, needs a second hand. Both hands are on the record; the key is in the shed. — Both parties, under the docket.

Services Weighing, rewriting, enforcing

118-S1

Notices rewritten in legible prose.

The hours are kept; the apology to the grammar is kept; the “whenever” is removed, or not, at the owner’s risk. The desk has hung the rehung notice, two lines, and can hang it again. Enforceability is not guaranteed: this court will not enforce a whenever, and neither will this desk. — The ads desk.

118-S2

Weighings, in the court yard.

By appointment, one fleece at a time, the scale of the court. The current mark is 2.71 kilograms (Exhibit C); the record fleece remains 3.9 in 2023, sold to the Baku mill at 14 manats the kilo, and the yard will go on believing it. The fix for the yard, as for the shed, is the one carried in Chapter 4 of Field Manual FM-9 — 40°33′N, 48°32′E to the minute — and a careless minute, at Goygol, is the difference between the shed and the plain, so the scale stands where it stands. — The Goygol station.

Notices From the division

118-N1

Fine, payable to the side.

From the Agricultural Division: the defendant, Nigar Hasanova, is fined in the amount of one fleece — 2.7 kilograms, weighed in fact 2.71 — payable to the Goygol station; the plaintiff, Elchin Veliyev, is in default of the notice as to the 18:40 lock and is ordered to rehang it in legible prose. Both parties are ordered to share the gate on a padlock, the key split. The judgment runs with the record, which is kept in triplicate: one copy in the office, one in Baku, and one, weather-permitting, on the bench in the shed. Full text at Case No. 44-41 of 2025. — Hon. Leyla Mammadova, for the record.

118-N2

The dairy season, by the numbers.

Milk is collected from the farm animals of the plain as an agricultural product, at dawn and, the ledger allowing, at the evening. What the column has posted so far this issue: 9.4 litres; one bucket; one fleece, 2.71 kilograms; one notice with a grammar of its own; 112 hens in their own coop, where they remain. The season reopens with the shed, on its own terms, Tuesday to Saturday. — The ads desk.

Elsewhere on the record

The full docket for the gate, the fleece and the notice is the excerpt from the verbatim record, Case No. 44-41 of 2025, Veliyev v. Hasanova, heard before the Hon. Leyla Mammadova on 4 November 2025 and closed at 11:40.

The fix this column posts at the top — 40°33′N, 48°32′E — is the same fix read back into that record, and it is worked out, angle by angle, in Chapter 4 of Field Manual FM-9, whose standing order to re-check the datum before you commit holds for a shed gate exactly as it holds for a reef.

The reciprocity the division applied to the cold gate is the sister court’s own law, applied there to a cold lavash in Case No. 1726 of 2024 at the Yerevan Municipal Court.
