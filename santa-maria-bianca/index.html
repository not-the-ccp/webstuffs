Regional Contingency Planning • Emilia-Romagna Sector

Santa Maria Bianca Hospital

Field Manual SM-B • Revision 4 • Mirandola, Province of Modena

1. Situation

Santa Maria Bianca Hospital is a community hospital in Mirandola, in the province of Modena, in Emilia-Romagna, Italy, standing on the ring road on the north side of town where the plain gives way to the Secchia river corridor. The main block dates from 1939 and carries 136 beds across four wards: Pronto Soccorso (emergency), Medicina, Maternità, and Riabilitazione. Radiology occupies the basement; the morgue is one flight below that. On 20 and 29 May 2012 the Emilia earthquakes cracked the east wing, and command moved into the container ward known as Sector C, where fifteen container units now hold general medicine and the pharmacy overflow. The roof helipad takes night landings on channel 146.425 MHz; the canal landing stage behind the kitchen serves the Sector C supply barge twice weekly.

The scenario assumes an incident at hour zero: a blackout, a blocked main road, and a full ward. Play proceeds from the admission desk at the Piazza dell'Ospedale entrance.

3. Roles

  • Medico — Dr. Elena Marconi or her deputy; decides treatment priority and signs death certificates.
  • Infermiere — ward staff; carries keys to the drug cupboard and the oxygen valves.
  • Staffetta — runner; moves between zones on foot, carries the message pad and whistle.
  • Civile — patient or visitor; may not enter Zone D or the roof without escort.

4. Procedure

Time is played in ten-minute turns. At the start of each turn the referee rolls one six-sided die for the incident table: power, water, aftershock, visitor, delivery, or silence. Codes are called over the ward radio: BIANCO for standby, ROSSO for mass casualties, NERO for abandonment of a zone. Evacuation of Ward North to Sector C takes three turns with a full evacuation-chair team; the morgue must be cleared before the stairs lock.

Each turn, every role may take one move and one action. The Medico treats one patient per turn; the Staffetta carries one message or item between any two zones; the Infermiere opens one locked cupboard or valve.

5. Field Kit and Standing Orders

Each role signs out a kit from the pharmacy safe: torch, two red tags, a water bottle, a pencil, and the sector map (scale 1:200, 1962 printing). The safe combination is posted inside the cupboard door of room 104.

Standing Order 9: Always have something in the cabin to drink and reel with me.

The cabin is Cà Rossa on the Secchia bank (Zone S), kept stocked with tinned food, dry wood, and a small boat moored at the landing stage. The reel is the cabin's spinning reel, used by night-watch players to keep their hands steady while the radio is silent. Drink keeps the voice clear; the reel keeps the hands busy. Enter the cabin only with the Medico's counter-sign, and leave it exactly as you found it: bottle recapped, line wound, lamp low.

When the helipad light is green, evacuation by air is possible: one stretcher or two seated players per flight, weather permitting. The scenario ends when all living patients reach Zone S or the helipad, or when the die turns up silence three turns running.
