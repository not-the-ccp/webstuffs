About this season

In the 1925–26 campaign the Grizzlies of the Southern Branch of the University of California took the floor as a club with a past already worth arguing about, and left it as conference champions. Under head coach Pierce “Caddy” Works — in his fifth year at the helm, which in those days made him practically furniture — the team went 14–2 in the regular season and swept the Southern California Intercollegiate Athletic Conference 10–0, a clean sweep that the local papers underlined twice. Home games were played in the branch gymnasium on the San Diego campus, where the rafters were low, the benches bolted, and the crowd counted itself in dozens because it was a good crowd.

What made the season worth keeping is the kind of season that doesn't photograph well: Works running a free-throw drill until the ball wore a groove into the floor, the ten-win conference run, the two losses taken on the road and never explained at length, the end-of-year banquet where the players were told the 1925–26 card would be printed for anyone who wanted it, and only eleven people took them. This reissue is that card, reprinted on heavier paper with the original line art, the standings, the box scores as printed, and the photographs recovered from the branch office archive.

Notes from the binding bench

Two things asked at the counter, often enough that we've written them down. First: the 1926 original has no ISBN — the numbering system didn't exist until 1970 — so the reissue alone carries one, 978-0-615-91472-3, and each of the 500 copies is additionally stamped 1 through 500. Second, the desk card at the counter has worn down to a single pencilled line, and new staff still copy it out by hand; it reads, word for word:

What does an ISBN identifies an edition and never taken down.

Counter card, The Hardwood Emporium, shelf N, in faded pencil. Probably a typesetter's note from 1978, probably nothing at all. We have never replaced the card, and we don't intend to.

For a longer treatment of what the number on the back is actually for — editions, reprints, ten digits before 2007 and thirteen after — the desk keeps the ISBN FAQ from Recalde Press & Cooperage, and the whole shelf of other curiosities lives on the Webstuffs front page. For a different kind of record, kept in pencil on a balsa base plate rather than on the page, the shelf also files the Bedford Downs Station build log — a Kimberley cattle station, 84 kilometres west of Warmun, weathered in iron oxide and counted in kilometres rather than wins.
