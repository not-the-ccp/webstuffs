NEPANTLA

Nahuatl n̂epantla · “in the middle of it” · “middle”  ·  synonym: nepantlayūllū (“the middlework”)  ·  Registry entry NP-13

OfficeOffice of Borderland Affairs, Xalostoc Annex, Room 214, Calle de la Orilla 13

KeepersThree nepantleras (those who walk the between), of whom the senior keeps the Room 214 register

1 Identification

Product name: Nepantla (n.). Nature: a concept of in-between-ness; a Nahuatl word meaning “in the middle of it” or “middle,” which may refer specifically to the space between two figurative or literal bodies of water. Uses as supplied: Chicano and Latino anthropology, social commentary, criticism, literature and art. In contemporary usage the substance most often refers to being between two cultures, particularly one’s original culture and the dominant one. Emergency: in a full submersion (see 11.3), call the crossing line and report which bank the subject has lost first.

2 Hazard identification

3 Composition / information on ingredients

Active constituent: 100% Nahuatl root, pre-Hispanic, Mesoamerican in origin. Carrier: the thirteen days of the nepantlayūllū, the nameless middle interval the old calendar set between the old year’s reckoning and the new year’s fire. Solvents of record: Chicano and Latino anthropology, social commentary, criticism, literature and art; the substance has been in continuous handling since the late twentieth century, its modern preparation owing most to the borderland literature of 1987, after which it is dispensed without further dilution.

4 First aid

Inhalation (acute): remove the affected person from the interval to a bench, if a bench is to be found; first aid is the bridge, not the bank. Skin contact: pain or loss may be reported, and the substance is sometimes used to designate exactly that state; keep the affected person warm with one story from each side, administered alternately. Ingestion: none known; the between is swallowed by reading. To be given to physician: the subject’s original culture, the dominant culture, and which of the two they have stopped answering first.

5 Firefighting

Not flammable as supplied. The thirteen-day carrier, however, dries the reeds at the water’s edge, and the altar fires of 1–2 November are lit from the interval itself. In that event use water taken from both banks in equal measure; extinguishing with a single culture’s water alone is recorded to spread the fire to the shore you did not intend to save.

6 Accidental release measures

If the concept spills into an official register, it will not evaporate; it will settle into a position of perspective, power, or potential and remain. Do not scrape it up. Sweep with a long reed and collect the sediment — which will include, invariably, the walkers themselves, the nepantleras — into a second container. Label the container “middle” and do not re-file it under either shore.

7 Handling and storage

Store between two bodies of water only. The site of record is the Sea of Marmara, the small inland sea lying between the Bosporus and the Dardanelles, where the substance was measured on 20 October in its purest field form — a double current, the fresher and lighter water running out at the surface while the saltier water runs in below, two directions at once (see Field Notes, Entry M-1, Sea of Marmara). Keep the reserve in Room 214, shelved between two ledgers, and keep it both dry and wet: the interval tolerates either shore’s weather provided the user cannot tell which shore the weather is coming from.

8 Exposure controls / personal protection

Two-way ventilation required at the reading desk. Personal protection: one passport of each direction; a reed for crossing; one story carried, at all times, in both directions. No user shall remain submerged on either shore for more than eight minutes — the reel allowance, per 14 — without returning once through the middle.

10 Stability and reactivity

Stable in in-between-ness under normal handling; unstable on either shore, where it hardens into a single culture and ceases to be this substance. Avoid prolonged contact with correction, emendation, or any office that would file the interval under one of its two neighbours. Incompatible materials: none named, by rule — the between takes what is given and makes it two-way.

11 Toxicological information

11.1 Acute: a position of perspective, power, or potential, usually within the first reading; no antidote, and none sought. 11.2 Chronic: double belonging; the habitual sense of being between two cultures, one’s original and the dominant. 11.3 Overdose: a state of pain or loss, the substance’s darker preparation — report it as such, without softening the entry, and keep the subject supplied, as in 4, with one story from each side.

12 Environmental information

The environment is itself the hazard: the substance disperses wherever two waters meet, and it cannot be removed from a river without removing the river’s middle. In the Marmara basin it was recorded in October at 17–18 °C water, riding the surface drift toward the Aegean while its heavy stratum ran the other way below. Non-biodegradable. Do not release into a single culture.

13 Disposal considerations

Not to be disposed of. The between is carried, whole, into the next ceremony; on 1–2 November it is declared seasonally and set out on the altars, where it is then handled freely and without a sheet, which is the one period of the year this office permits. Thereafter the reserve returns to Room 214 between its two ledgers.

14 Transport information

The substance is carried unsealed, which is to say, as shipped. Its manifest rides on the term report of Room 214, Form 3B, and the following line of that report is transcribed word for word, as received, and not corrected at all:

The method of conducting a presidential election and is not corrected at all, and it runs eight minutes — see Galileo — Term Report, Room 214 .

The register reads the line thus: “it” is the demonstration reel of the procedure in question, and the reel runs eight minutes; “see Galileo” points to Exhibit G, the working model of the double current hung in Room 214, where two streams are shown running opposite ways in the same vessel; and the clause “is not corrected at all” is the registrar’s standing refusal to emend the manifest, which is the rule of this office. The same unamended line keeps the collection’s other Form 3B sheet — the base hit report of Room 214 (The Base Hit — Term Report, Form 3B) — which holds the left-field corner at Comiskey exactly where it was thrown, and which the river letter of the Asunción Ledger (Letter No. 27) cites in the same uncorrected spirit, reading a worn waterway line whole because the river, like a dead man’s log, does not stop to be fixed.

15 Regulatory information

Classified as an intermediate substance under the standing accords of the Office; seasonal declaration required 1–2 November (the day the middle is named and the altars opened). Registry entry NP-13, reviewed each year during the thirteen days of the nepantlayūllū. Sister sheets in the record family — the Lagan single of 1995 (Safety Data Sheet No. 1995-05-15) and the sealed 164-day season filed beside the Asunción letter — govern their own substances and are cited here only as precedent for clause-by-clause reading.

16 Other information

This sheet is read clause by clause, the way the Lagan sheet is read and the Room 214 manifest is read, and it ends where the water between deepens. Full collection, all shelves and both banks: return to the front page.
