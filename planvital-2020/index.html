4 First-Aid Measures

  • On exposure to a late equaliser: move the spectator to a seat, loosen the scarf, and hold the arms down until the pulse returns to resting rate. Do not argue with the television; it has already shown the replay.
  • On exposure to a 0–0 draw on 10 February 2021: for Universidad Católica supporters the symptom is the quietest of the season — the fifteenth title secured with a game to spare. No treatment required; a beer is indicated and legally available after the final whistle.
  • On sustained relegation-zone exposure: refer to a supporter of a club in the top half. Do not mix with a supporter of the club below yours.

5 Fire-Fighting

Combustible material: scarves, flags, pyrotechnics at away ends. The Union La Calera end has been catalogued as an open furnace all season and should be treated as such by the fire service. Use water, not water: the difference matters in February, when the temperature in Santiago passes 30°C and the match starts at 20:30. Extinguishing is the job of the stewards of the ANFP; the public's only duty is to find the exit marked in both Chilean and Spanish, which is to say, twice.

6 Accidental Release (Suspension, 18 Mar – 29 Aug 2020)

On 18 March 2020 the product was released from controlled operation in the event of the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic and confined for 164 days. Containment was effective: no goals, no transfers of note, no standings movement. On 29 August 2020 the batch was re-contained behind closed doors and resumed with 20 matchdays remaining; the compressed back half of the season should be regarded as a pressure event. Do not open the sealed period: it will not reopen, and the fixtures inside it are forever played in an empty Estadio Nacional.

7 Handling & Storage

Handle the product only on matchdays, and only near a ground. Around the San Carlos de Apoquindo, the match flows out into the streets of Ñuñoa and Camino Apóstol Santiago fills with scarves an hour before kickoff and again for an hour after. Wear comfortable shoes; the sidewalks are original hexagonal pavers in places, uneven and slick after rain. Store the season itself in a cool mind: a full 38 rounds, compressed into a back half played without spectators, does not keep well in the same head twice.

Do not store near a calendar of the 2019–20 season; cross-contamination of dates is common and the two cannot be told apart after the third round.

8 Exposure Control / Personal Protection

  • Occupational exposure limit (OEL): 90 minutes + stoppage per match; 38 matchdays per season. No overtime is permitted; the league enforces this strictly.
  • Required PPE: club scarf (primary), hat for away trips north, rain shell for Concepción, patience for Iquique.
  • Household limit: one football match per evening. A second exposure on the same night is an accident, not a schedule, and must be reported to the family before the post-match programme.

10 Stability and Reactivity

The batch is stable under normal conditions of a Chilean February. Conditions to avoid: a penalty shoot-out, a fourth official's extended stoppage, and any claim that Unión La Calera "only" finished runners-up, which the club of La Calera files a formal objection against. Incompatible with: the calendar of 2019–20, the schedule of 2021, and anyone who asks why the season was not restarted in April.

11 Toxicology

Chronic exposure to a season in which the champions were crowned a round early produces in the rest of the table a mild, long-lasting, treatable envy. Acute exposure to Universidad Católica's three-in-a-row (14th, 15th and 16th counting from the start, but fifteenth in league titles) is associated with a temporary loss of belief in the 19-club model, which recovers in May. Long-term prognosis for the product: excellent; the 90th season of the league ends with its oldest member, Universidad de Chile, and its newest, the young La Calera, finishing top two — a finding the safety board found remarkable. The oldest member has a standing entry of its own in this collection's dreamer's concordance, under the blue: the entry for Universidad de Chile, which records eighteen league titles, the crest adopted in 1943, and a good omen for any sleeper who has watched the Nacional stand dark.

12 Ecological Considerations

A runners-up finish has a measurable effect on a town. La Calera sits in the Limarí valley in the Coquimbo Region, a copper and fruit-growing district of some 17,000 people, and a second-place finish in the national league was registered there as an event of roughly the size of the harvest. Dispose of the season responsibly; it biodegrades over one transfer window in an ordinary household memory.

13 Disposal Considerations

After the relegation play-off on 17 February 2021, dispose of the remaining standings by the standard method: let one of the two bottom clubs take the whole thing to the second division, and bury the fixture list with the calendar. Do not incinerate in the presence of a third-division club's supporters; they are entitled to the smoke.

14 Transport Information

The product travelled the length of Chile in 2020–21: from the port of Arica to the south end of a country shaped like a finger, with scheduled away fixtures at Concepción (Huachipato's Coloso), Iquique (the beach city on the border), Coquimbo and La Serena (the northern run), Talca (O'Higgins), Chillán (Ñublense) and the two Santiago addresses — the Nacional and the San Carlos. Transport classification: passenger traffic, high-density, no exceptions in a derby. No hazmat placard is required, but a scarf is, at either end. For the rail-minded reader, a comparable line's full working sheet is kept in this collection: the fifth edition of the timetable for Service TC–7, the Toothcomb Limited, Antananarivo to Etosha Halt, whose commentator likewise refuses to let any departure pass without a remark.

15 Regulatory Information

Regulated by the ANFP for the Chilean Football Federation and operated by the Liga de Fútbol Profesional; sponsorship by AFP PlanVital is a commercial contract, not a protective measure, though the name is required on all posters. The suspension of 18 March – 29 August 2020 was a compliance requirement under the state of public health emergency; no waiver was filed, and none was granted. This sheet is issued for information only and does not create a warranty for any club's final position.
