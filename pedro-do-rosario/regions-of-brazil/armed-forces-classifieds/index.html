TAKEN IN FULL THIS WEEK BY THE FORÇAS ARMADAS BRASILEIRAS

The wire

— the wire is quiet, the flats are running out —

Notice to readers: the classifieds page is taken up this week by the Forças Armadas Brasileiras, the unified military forces of the Federative Republic of Brazil. The three service branches — the Brazilian Army, the Brazilian Navy and the Brazilian Air Force — each filed its copy before dawn and have not yet agreed which of them signs the page. Their count stands at 334,500 active-duty troops and officers, the second largest in the Americas after the United States and the largest in Latin America and the Southern Hemisphere by the level of military equipment. The Ledger does not verify counts, but it prints them free of charge, and it prints everything else at a centavo and a half the word.

Sold 3 listings

No. 1432–01

ONE (1) CASK of delta rum, cask-aged, ex-naval store, sound butt, no leak except one. Tastes of low water and salt. Buyers may request a taste and the cask has been requested. The best are from a cask near by. — Naval Stores, by way of Rua da Paz

No. 1432–02

ARMY SURVEYOR'S COMPASS, 1971, last fixed on the Pedra do Rosário rock, which shows in the flats at low water; the bearing in across the flats is held in chapter four of the field manual on the geographic coordinate system. Sold, against its own protest, to the club for safekeeping; the club will not confirm the sale and asks that no one be asked.

No. 1432–03

THREE (3) SERVICE BRANCHES, Army, Navy, Air Force, offered separately or as a lot; they will not be separated and will not say why. Lot sold to the delta at low water, collect all three together or not at all. — Auctioneers of the mangrove

Lost 3 listings

No. 1432–04

ONE (1) SIGNAL WHISTLE, naval, tin, last heard at the 2017 send-off for Haiti, where Brazilian soldiers led the United Nations Stabilization Mission (MINUSTAH) from 2004 until 2017. It has been answered twice from the mangrove. Reward: one shell from the flats. The Ledger suspects the answering belongs to Seu Benedito, who does not answer whistles.

No. 1432–05

ONE (1) SANDAL, air force, one of a pair, worn across the Atlantic and believed to have come down over the delta. The pair is second in the Americas, after the United States, and first in Latin America and the Southern Hemisphere by the level of military equipment, if not by footwear. The other sandal remains on the shelf and will not speak of it.

No. 1432–06

ONE (1) PARADE DATE, misplaced. Last seen about 10 June, when the square already had a claim on it; Bumba-meu-boi de Orquestra holds it under rope-tuned drums and will return it for a share of the square. The club declines to referee. — Lost Property, under the mango tree

Wanted 1 listing

No. 1432–07

JURISDICTION OF THE MOUTH AT LOW WATER. The three branches, Army, Navy, Air Force, have each filed a claim; the club has voted twice to keep the question open, after the misbound surveyor's page that married the shoreline to the coordinates, and will keep it open a third time if asked. Offers by letter, to the mango tree, marked for the mouth, not the rock.

For hire 1 listing

No. 1432–08

DRUM TUNER, rope, by the mangrove. Available to the military bands and to Bumba-meu-boi de Orquestra on a strict rotation; the square takes one at a time, from 10 June, and the rope takes no bribes. Terms: one shell from the flats, or a number between one and twenty-six. — Seu Benedito, farinha house

Personal 3 listings

No. 1432–09

THE OBITUARY of Regiões do Brasil, filed in the Costa Aberta Forum, is printed in full at Regiões do Brasil — Obituary; the family asks for nothing in lieu of flowers, which is a term this page knows how to honour. Memorial ads may be placed on the same terms: one candle each, one per page load. No flowers will be run. She never had political effects.

No. 1432–10

DONA LUZIA has learned the cabinet ministers in alphabetical order ahead of the feast of São Pedro and will recite them while shelling peas; the list, and everything else in the federation read from the delta, is set out in The Federative Letter, Newsletter No. 14. Recitations may be heard under the mango tree, Tuesdays, or when the tide permits. — A reader, Rua da Paz

No. 1432–11

TO THE READER of Pedro do Rosário — anyone been?: the fare from São Luís is unchanged. The Navy holds the mouth at low water, the Air Force holds the high water, the Army holds the square, and all three are reachable by letter, which is the delta's only honest form of transport.
