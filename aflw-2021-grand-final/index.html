EAF-21-002 · GATE RESOLVED 12:41

Ticketing: "QUEUE FULL — SOUTH STAND LIFT" (raised 12:06)

This is the match this documentation belongs to. The 2021 AFL Women's Grand Final decided the premiers of the fifth season of the AFLW competition, and the field was contested by the Adelaide Crows and the Brisbane Lions — the second grand final between the two clubs after the 2017 Grand Final, which Adelaide won, and each side's third grand final appearance, following Adelaide's premierships in 2017 and 2019 and Brisbane's losses in the 2017 and 2018 grand finals. It was the first AFLW grand final since 2019, the pause in the calendar having stretched the build to a two-year arc that the ticketing module was never sized for. The queue error at the South Stand lift cleared in four minutes once the second turnstile bank was brought online; no patron of the day's 22,679 was turned from a purchased seat, and the gate ledger closed clean.

EAF-21-066 · SCORE RESOLVED 1:12 PM

Score clock: "HALF-TIME NOT REACHED — DO NOT PUBLISH" (raised 12:58)

The published score for the fixture, retained here so the documentation is not orphaned from its result: Brisbane 17.14 (116), Adelaide 11.9 (75), a 41-point margin at the final siren of the afternoon. The mid-session figures had Brisbane 18 points clear, the margin quoted throughout the day, and the lead held to the last over. The win was the Lions' first senior women's premiership; Adelaide's third grand final and second championship of the young competition did not come back. The hold on publication fired because the scoreboard clock ran 14 seconds slow in the second quarter; the match official's clock was confirmed as the master and the public screens were corrected before the second quarter closed.

EAF-21-090 · CONSOLE WORKING AS INTENDED

Announcement system: "CHANNEL 3 BUSY — MEMORIAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT" (raised 2:05 PM)

Not a fault; logged for completeness. The day carried an extended pre-game memorial acknowledgement, and the PA held Channel 3 for its full duration ahead of the match commentary feed. Operators in the broadcast box on the members' side worked to the master cue sheet; the acknowledgment ran three minutes long, the 3:20 first kick was kept to the minute, and the Lions led 4.2 (26) to 2.5 (17) at the end of the opening term with the Oval's south stand in full colour.

EAF-21-171 · CATERING UNRESOLVED — RETAINED AS WRITTEN

Kiosk 9 (East Terrace): "PLATE RECORD VOID — SMALL-PRINT APPLIES" (raised 5:47 PM)

The only entry in this documentation that the systems could not clear, and it is preserved here exactly as it was read into the post-match debrief, because the audit rule is that an unexplained record is kept, not erased. The kiosk's own log explains the state it was found in:

The blank plate held by a body in which it has waited out the landslide of 1960, which the menu notes, in small print.

The line refers to the venue's history: the 1960 landslide that took the corner of the Oval's eastern grandstand, and the restoration works that sealed the site; Kiosk 9 stands on the restored terrace, and the small print on the menu is a line of provenance, not an allergy notice. The plate record itself was void in the catering module — the day's last service had reconciled to the day before — so the kiosk closed its till at 5:47 PM and was reopened the following morning with a clean plate register. The Lions' premiership walkover was on the field by then, the Crows' players on the oval for the final time of the afternoon, and no patron was affected.

EAF-21-208 · CLOSE-OUT SEALED 11:30 PM

Day-end: "BUILD 5.17.2021 ARCHIVED — PREMIERS: BRISBANE LIONS"

The season's final fixture closed the competition at 11:30 PM after the grounds crew cleared the oval and the trophy was handed over in the presentation circle. Brisbane's first senior women's premiership was entered into the permanent record at 11:31 PM, four years after the Lions' second grand-final loss and two years after Adelaide's second premiership of the competition. Every system in the build reported green at close-out; this page is the last thing the 2021 grand final build writes.
