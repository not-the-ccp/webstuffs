Role-Playing Classifieds

Vol. 1 · No. 3 · Printed on the reverse of a chile recipe card · Clayton · Waitematā · Burt County

A role-playing game is a game in which players assume and play out the roles of characters in a fictional setting, taking responsibility for acting out those roles within a narrative — through literal acting or through structured decision-making about character development — and in many of them actions succeed or fail according to a formal system of rules and guidelines. The original form, the tabletop role-playing game, is conducted through discussion. Below is the current register: games seeking players, dice for sale, live action notices, a wanted lining, and an editor who will not retrain from scratch.

01Players and Games Wanted

Sixth Player — Rim World Campaign, Thursdays

Experienced players only. The table meets Thursdays, 7:00–11:00 p.m., in the back room of the motel on Main Street, Clayton, New Mexico — the same room where Luz de la Fuente waited out a failed alternator in the summer of 1987 and read Santiago: A Myth of the Far Future aloud to the owner's daughter while the cook, Esperanza Reyes, listened from the kitchen door. The chile she cooked that winter is written up as Santiago's Trail Chile — A Family Recipe, and the campaign still runs on it: beef stew is provided, and every story must grow taller with every telling.

Apply in writing to Luz de la Fuente, care of the chile card, Denver, Colorado. Mention the ancho.

Game Master Wanted — Saturday Afternoons, Waitematā

A group on the Auckland isthmus seeks a game master for a fortnightly Saturday game set in the creative offices above a former clinic building. Players: four, plus one empty chair. Venue: the ground-floor waiting room, Graham Street Community Room, 12 Graham Street, Auckland Central, when the board is not in session. No experience required; willingness to keep a register of immunisations in-character is a plus.

Post to the board secretary, care of Waitematā Local Board minutes, WLB/2024/EM-06.

02For Sale

Jonas Fitch, Section 14, Bell Creek, Burt County, Nebraska. Serious inquiries only; the novel stays with the seller.

03Live Action

LARP — The Register, Saturday 14 June

Live action role-play on the Waitematā isthmus: players physically perform the personae of register clerks, a data stewardship officer on sabbatical, and a board secretary, in the ground-floor waiting room of the former Newton Road clinic building, 12 Graham Street, Auckland Central. 2:00–6:00 p.m. Costume: cream cardigans, clipboards, sensible shoes. Dice are expressly forbidden; all resolution is by conversation and the register. Organized by Ofanaite Ana Dewes, whose submission to the Waitematā Local Board minutes asked that the register be transferred to the Pacific Health Collection rather than destroyed.

RSVP to Elise Moreau, Board Secretary. Cream cardigans only.

05Lost and Found

Lost — One Carpenter's Whale Book

A copy of Moby-Dick; or, The Whale, American first issue binding, 1851, with carpenter's pencil marks in the early chapters and a coffee ring, still pale, across the chapter on the whale's size. Last seen in the possession of Jonas Fitch in October 1899, at the Bell Creek one-room schoolhouse, Burt County, Nebraska. Fitch was “in the midst of it”; the roof went unrepaired, two summonses went unanswered, and two Saturdays were missed.

Reward: two Saturdays, or the roof, whichever arrives first. Claims to Hartwell & Gann, Auctioneers, 1208 Thirteenth Street, Lincoln, Nebraska — Lot 27, the Bell Creek Township Papers. The box is walnut and brass-lined; bring both Saturdays.
