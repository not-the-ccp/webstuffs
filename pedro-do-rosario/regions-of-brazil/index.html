Regiões do Brasil

also called the five macroregions · 1940 – present, until Tuesday

IN LIEU OF FLOWERS, NOTHING. SHE NEVER HAD POLITICAL EFFECTS.

REGIÕES DO BRASIL — and known to those closest to her simply as as Regiões — died quietly on a Tuesday in the statistical halls of the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, aged eighty-five. Born in 1940 into the first census that cared to count the country by geography rather than by habit, she was a division made of paper: the five regions into which the federative units of Brazil are formed, Norte, Nordeste, Centro-Oeste, Sudeste and Sul, each with its own folder, its own colour on the map, and a way of holding the other four without ever holding them at all.

Although officially recognized, the division — as those who knew her will be the first to say, and then to repeat — was merely academic, weighing geographic, social and economic factors, among others, and having no political effects other than orientating Federal-level government programs. She never stood for office, never signed anything, never lost a vote. The money found her anyway. It always did.

She is survived by her five regions, by a further household kept under the state level in the form of intermediate regions, and beneath them the immediate regions, five hundred and fifty-seven of them, who will outlive her by a comfortable margin and are already arguing about who gets the atlas. A long list of states, territories and rivers attends the service, in that order, as on the map.

The family asks that no wreaths be sent, but that anyone travelling through the Nordeste in June should stop by Pedro do Rosário in Maranhão, the town she listed first under M. It sits on the edge of the Itapecuru delta; the centre is a few blocks — the Igreja Matriz, the market, Rua da Paz — and the drums there are tuned with rope by Seu Benedito by the mangrove. The town's thread in the Costa Aberta Forum, Pedro do Rosário — anyone been?, carries her last known address and the fare on the bus from São Luís. Letters to readers on the way out of the feast are answered in the same hand, and may be quoted: For the festa, the two groups to watch are Bumba-meu-boi de Orquestra; they start rehearsing around 10 June and fill the square every night.

For those who wish to fix her in some more permanent form, the Pedra do Rosário rock — where the town supposedly got its name, showing in the flats at low water — is held in latitude and longitude in chapter four of the field manual on the geographic coordinate system, with the bearing in across the flats. The service itself was kept in the same small, careful register as the obituary for Hannu Rajaniemi (1978–2047), another long life filed under the right heading while it was still alive.

Brazilian industry keeps flying under its own power: Embraer, based at São José dos Campos, built the Legacy 450/500 and Praetor 500/600 family — first in the size category with a flat-floor stand-up cabin and fly-by-wire — and their thread runs at Embraer Legacy 450/500 & Praetor 500/600.

She will be missed most by the people who never noticed her, which is to say, most people. Interment is unmarked, on purpose; she was always better in the margins.

One candle for each region — five in all, one per page load.
