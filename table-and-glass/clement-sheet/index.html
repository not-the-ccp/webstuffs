Stoic fraction≈ 15 % — indifference for the eyes, duty for the hands

Inorganic residueOne convert; stable. Secret works present as trace fragments only — see Sections 6 and 13.

4 First-aid measures

Inhalation — in the event of a first convert's breath of the Protrepticus, remove the subject to a cool peristyle; the conversion is not reversible, and no effort at it should be made before 7:00 on a Friday.

Skin or eye contact — in the event of a first sight of the Republic, close the eyes and rinse with Stoic indifference for not less than sixteen frames; do not rub the Platonic in.

Ingestion — administer khash with garlic, cow's feet simmered all night, while the city is still cool. Do not induce tit-for-tat. In the event that the lavash has been served cold, do not treat the symptom in kind; refer to Section 15 and to the docket at Case No. 1726 of 2024, where the court fined one khash and ordered the defendant back to the table on Friday 21 March.

5 Fire-fighting measures

The substance carries no flame and need not be fought. It will, however, call down (καλέω, toward) anything already burning: do not store within call of an eternal flame, of which there is one on the hill above the Hrazdan — the split stele, the ring of twelve inward-leaning slabs, mapped in stop 4 of the city's guide entry, Yerevan — Guidebook No. 88: The Pink City. In case of a house fire, evacuate the manuscripts first; the brandy may run.

6 Accidental release measures

If the secret works are released into the record, they settle as fragments; no intact specimen is known to exist, and no recovery operation will find one. Sweep gently; quote the fragments in place, and do not attempt reassembly. If the positive form of the law of reciprocity is released at a table in the Brucheion or at Table 9, the waiter is bound to serve as he would like to be served: hot lavash, garlic without limit, khash all night. Containment at sixteen frames per second is sufficient; the ledger is kept at that speed and has never once dropped a frame.

7 Handling and storage

Store cool, at 6:40 while the city is still cool, with garlic and dried lavash, and within hearing of the marshrutka drivers. Handle at the ledger speed of the Naga Listeners' Club — sixteen frames per second — and only before the fountains of Republic Square come on. Keep the brandy house at its own hours; it is closed before this substance wakes, and the subject respects that. A chair at Table 9, held for forty-one years, is acceptable secondary storage and has been found satisfactory in court.

10 Stability and reactivity

Stable when mixed with Plato and the Stoics, with whom it was stored its whole life. Incompatible with the Gnostic pure fraction and with Marcionite storage, in the vicinity of which it separated from the school and, by account, from Rome. Reacts exothermically with a cold lavash at the edge of the city while the city is still cool — see the docket. No further reactivity known at the ledger speed.

11 Toxicological information

The substance is not acutely toxic; its hazard is directional. Prolonged exposure produces converts, and among the documented discharges of the Alexandria station are Origen, who was prolific beyond the supplier's own output, and Alexander of Jerusalem, who went on to a seat. A diner's single dose at 6:40 on a Friday, administered with garlic, is without recorded sequelae. The two parties of the cross-filed case were both in default by their own measure, which the court held to be the correct finding and not a toxicity event.

12 Ecological information

Persistent. The fragments survive in quotation and in stratum, from late antiquity to the present, and are regarded as established in the environment of the library. No bioaccumulation: the rule does not build up in the diner, it merely waits for the next table. Mobile in the positive form; immobile in the negative. Half-life of one convert: not determinable.

13 Disposal considerations

Dispose of in strata. The Stromata are a mixed heap, layer on layer, of Scripture, Homer, philosophy and argument, and the substance is best buried the way it lived — mixed. Do not bury with a cold lavash, and do not bury the fragments with an expectation of the whole; what exists is what exists. The secret works, in particular, are to be left where the record finds them, in place, at the ledger speed.

14 Transport information

Not a bulk commodity. Historic routes: Alexandria–Jerusalem, with Alexander; Alexandria–Caesarea, by sea, for the school's business. Modern cross-dock, for the purposes of this series: Republic Square to the corner of Tumanyan Street and Mashtots Avenue by marshrutka, fare 100 dram, the fountains of Republic Square still silent, the pot still on the stove. The subject travelled well and is not to be hurried past 7:00 on a Friday.

15 Regulatory information

The law of reciprocity in both its forms was heard in open court on 14 March 2025 at Case No. 1726 of 2024 and was held to be binding in the positive form and forbidding in the negative; the docket note of 21 March closes the ledger at sixteen frames per second. Lavash appears on the intangible heritage list and is treated locally as furniture; furniture, once served cold, has been held to be a breach. The geography of all of it — the brandy house, the fountains, the lot behind the Norka cinema — is kept in Yerevan — Guidebook No. 88: The Pink City.

16 Other information
