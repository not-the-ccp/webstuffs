★ On the record

What you are holding is not a press release. It is the sleeve of CCDI-001, Inner Regulations, the Red Seal Sessions, cut on quarter-inch tape at seven and a half inches per second in the listening room above the reception hall at 1 Fuyou Street, Xicheng District, Beijing — a block from Zhongnanhai, off West Chang'an Avenue. The commission on this record is the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection of the Chinese Communist Party: elected by the National Congress and supervised by it between congresses, charged with defending the party constitution, enforcing inner-party regulations, coordinating anti-corruption work, and, as its highest responsibility, safeguarding the core position of the general secretary and of the Central Committee as a whole. Because officials at nearly every level of government carry party membership, the commission is, in practice, the country's senior anti-corruption body.

The album follows one inspection circuit: the opening plenum in the Great Hall of the People, the two xunxi circuits through the provinces, the rooms where tigers and flies are processed alike, the Skynet and Fox Hunt returns from overseas, and one evidence tape, sealed in wax, marked No. 4. Running time about forty-four minutes. Keep the receipt.

★ Notes from the label

This commission keeps its minutes the way a yard keeps a timetable: in ink, in sequence, and with no run missing. Two other ledgers travel with this one. Furio Piccirilli's marble register at Mott Haven, where the Piccirilli Brothers cut Washington's seated Lincoln from twenty-eight blocks of Georgia stone, is kept at The Marble Limited, Timetable & Commentary. A field naturalist's notes from the Green Mountains — Ethan Allen, his boys, and Fort Ticonderoga — are kept at Ethan Allen — Field Notes. Both are kept at Webstuffs, and both keep their own reckoning.

The master tape is held in four parts. Part one: the plenum. Part two: the circuit. Part three: the return. No. 4 — the part I keep, Your Honor. — sits in the duty officer's drawer, sealed in red wax, awaiting its next hearing.
