The Grace Line · Service G · Grace Award for Most Inspiring Performance

Working Timetable, 74th Edition · in force from the 115th season of the awards · published from the station office at Rialto Junction

Preliminary Notes

Service G runs once a year, in the spring, when the voting returns come in and the envelopes are read aloud at the platform. The award itself is old and plain in its purpose: Movieguide gives the Grace Award for the most inspiring performances in movies and television, and the railway has kept pace with it since the first ceremony, running one scheduled service per ceremony, no more, because one inspiring performance a year is all the line can be certified to carry. Trains travel from Rialto Junction — the studio gate, where the nominees arrive as five candidates and are counted on the finger of the platform porter — to Inspiration Quay, one platform out, where the winner boards and the line ends for the season. The intermediate stations are named for the winners of the intervening years, and the timetable below is the line as it stands, with the commentary in the right-hand column, as the signalman enters it.

Passengers are reminded that the service is not express: it stops at every winner, and a train that passes a winner's platform is a train the Company disavows. The one regularity of the Grace Line is that it always arrives, and it always carries somebody, and the Somebody is named in the register before the last carriage leaves. For the manner of the line's commentary — stop by stop, from the platform to the paddock — the Company refers passengers to the Symonds Express timetable, the sister service on which the Grace Line's signal box has long been modelled; and for the older, wordier tradition of station registers, to the Form 4B report on Mabel's Married Life, where eighteen edits in thirteen minutes were accounted for to the last gasolier.

The Signalman's Ledger

The station office at Rialto Junction keeps one ledger a season, in which the signalman enters the times at which the line opened, the platforms at which the doors stayed open, and such lines as arrive from elsewhere and do not parse. The ledger of the 74th season is in the safe with the voting returns, and the Company prints here the only entry that is not a time, exactly as the signalman wrote it, with the pencil marks left in: “I am asked to transcribe verbatim, with or you are in my hands.” It has been read, at the office, as an instruction to the signalman — that the award, whatever its manner, is a thing that is handed over bodily, in the same way the timetable itself is handed over to the passengers each March — and the Company is content with that reading, because the alternative reading is worse, and because the line, on either reading, has never once failed to arrive. The ledger's full register is held at the quay, and the reader who wishes to compare a line kept word for word with a line kept word for word in a different office may consult the Form 4B report on Mabel's Married Life, which keeps its gasoliers the same way.

Fares, and the Fine Print

Fare on Service G is one nomination, payable at Rialto Junction, and one nomination is accepted from each candidate, in the person, at the window, between 08.00 and 08.03. There is no return ticket; the line terminates at the quay by design, because an inspiring performance is not a place the line visits twice and leaves better than it found, but a place the line ends at, once, and is measured by. Concessions: the crew of the year's films travel free, and the platform porter's family, four of them, travel free every season, and no one on the line will say on whose account that last concession is, though the ledger in the office holds the account and the account, as the signalman writes of it, is kept in pencil, and in pencil it will stay. The Company reserves the right to name a platform before the vote is counted, as at Tandy Terrace, and the right, once named, to keep the name for as long as the performance stands. Changes, if changes there be, appear in the next edition of this timetable, one year hence, at 08.04, when the line opens and the register is read aloud at the platform.
