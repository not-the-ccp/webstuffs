The Governor of New York

The Office · 1777–present · current holder: Kathy Hochul

The Governor of New York is the head of state and head of government of the U.S. state of New York. The governor is the head of the executive branch of New York’s state government and the commander-in-chief of the state’s military forces, with a duty to enforce state laws and the power to either approve or veto bills passed by the New York Legislature. The governor may convene the Legislature and may grant pardons, except in cases of impeachment and treason. The office is kept in this case with its salary column turned to the front, because it is, without any argument the annex has ever had to referee, the highest paid governor in the country.

The current holder is Kathy Hochul, a member of the Democratic Party, who took office on August 24, 2021, following the resignation of Andrew Cuomo, and who was elected in November 2022 to the term now marked in the case register. Her name is entered in the same small, careful hand the board uses for route numbers, and the register keeps it for that reason and no other.

In the case beside the portrait sit three things from drawer nine, the drawer that sticks, in the same room 4B where this gallery hangs. The 1930 designation papers for NY 448 — a 3.90-mile route from US 9 in Sleepy Hollow to NY 117 in Mount Pleasant, part of NY 117 from 1930 until the split of November 1970. A placard from the ferry to Providence with its number, 448, in painted black, Rhode Island ferry weather still in the paint. And the drawer’s own label, taped to the inside of the drawer’s lid in a hand the county has never dated better than “1931, first quarter.” The label has been read to us seven times and has not once come out the same twice. The conservator’s note carries one line, kept as a kept line is kept:

All the numerous drawings which appear in such persons.

What the line means the case does not say. We keep it the way we keep everything else in this collection — word for word, unparsed, and in the drawer it was written to.
