The Rental

2020 · American horror film · Co-written, produced and directed by Dave Franco · Screenplay by Dave Franco and Joe Swanberg · Released by IFC Films, July 24, 2020

Synopsis

Two couples — Charlie (Dan Stevens) and Michelle (Alison Brie), and Mina (Sheila Vand) with her boyfriend Josh (Jeremy Allen White), Charlie's younger brother — rent an oceanfront house on the Oregon coast for a weekend getaway. The property is managed by the curt, watchful Taylor (Toby Huss). After Mina finds a hidden camera behind a mirror in the guest cottage, the four guests begin to suspect they are being watched in the house they rented.

Production

The Rental was the feature directorial debut of Dave Franco, who co-wrote the screenplay with Joe Swanberg from a story by Franco, Swanberg, and Mike Demski. Principal photography ran for twenty-four days in the summer of 2019 on the Oregon coast, chiefly around Newport, with the rental house erected as a practical set above the beach. Christian Sprenger photographed the film on 35 mm, and Danny Bensi and Saunder Jurriaans supplied the score. The principal cast rehearsed together for a week before shooting so that the friendships were already frayed when the cameras arrived.

Release

The film was released on video-on-demand (VOD) and in select theaters in the United States on July 24, 2020, by IFC Films. It debuted atop the domestic VOD chart while playing on fewer than forty theatrical screens, becoming the second film ever to top the VOD charts and the box office in the same weekend.

Reception

Reviews were generally positive. Critics praised Franco's restraint, the coiled tension of the film's first hour, and the performances of the quartet, though some found the final act more conventional than what had gone before. Rotten Tomatoes records an approval rating of seventy-five per cent; Metacritic assigns a score of sixty-one. The American Film Institute later cited it among the stronger genre debuts of the year (American Film Institute — A Letter to the Editor).
