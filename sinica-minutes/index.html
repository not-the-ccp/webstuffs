Minutes of a Special Meeting

Ref. TS/CC/2024/11-14 · Sealed & countersigned · Page 1 of 2

DateThursday, 14 November 2024
HeldAt 19:00, in the second-floor committee room, Rinko gatvė 17, Vilnius (the room with the green curtains and the window onto the fountain)
PresentAndrius Kairys (Chairman); Vytautas Sinica (Vice-Chairman); Rūta Petrauskienė (Secretary); Darius Vilkas (Treasurer); Eglė Ramonienė; Tomas Šimkus; Neringa Balčiūnaitė; Kęstutis Urbonas
AbsentJustas Petraitis (on medical leave, letter of apology on the table)
MinutesTaken by R. Petrauskienė, read and confirmed at the next sitting

  1. 1. Chairing and Quorum

    The Chairman, Mr. Andrius Kairys, called the meeting to order at 19:04 after a brief exchange about the radiator. Eight members being present and two absent with apologies, a quorum was declared. The minutes of the previous meeting, of 22 August, were read through and approved without amendment.

  2. 2. Election of 4 October 2024 — Return of the Result

    Mr. Vytautas Sinica, vice-chairman of the Alliance and a political scientist by training, gave the Council the return. In the 2024 Lithuanian parliamentary election he stood in the party’s single-seat constituency and was returned to the Seimas, becoming the first member of the Seimas ever elected from the National Alliance. The figures as read from the tally sheet: a two-year-old party entering parliament for the first time, its name now printed in the official gazette alongside names the gazette has carried since 1916. The Council was asked to note the result, and noted it, with the applause recorded in the margin of these minutes in the Secretary’s hand.

    Mr. Sinica, speaking from the head of the table by the window, thanked the volunteers of the autumn campaign, named the door-to-door brigades in the east of the city by street, and asked that no thank-you speech be printed in the party paper “because it will be wrong and I will be embarrassed”. It was agreed.

  3. 3. First Parliamentary Group — Rooms and Stationery

    The Treasurer read the group’s first budget. There is no group to join, the Council heard, and so the rooms must be taken: a desk and a small office on the first floor of the Seimas, to be furnished in January. The motion carried, 7–0, that the group be established with an initial grant of the sum agreed with the Seimas administration, that the desk be of dark wood rather than laminate, and that the stationery carry the full party name, not the abbreviation, “for the first time”.

  4. 4. Travel and Expenses — The London Note

    Mr. Vilkas moved that the travel claims of the autumn be approved, and read the one item the Council should see before approving, from Mr. Sinica’s own written note appended to his claim for the London leg of the conference in late September. The note concerned the state of the bus journey into Hammersmith on the morning of the keynote address, and reads, in Mr. Sinica’s hand:

    “Having made the journey four times, I am able to report the following. The 07:15 at Godolphin & Latymer, Hammersmith, is a small island at the 2018 count. Four crossings a morning, one shelter with a missing panel, and a route that treats the whole of the south side of the river as an optional extra. For those who do not know London, a small island is a place you can see the whole of in a single glance; the 07:15 is that. I arrived late to the keynote, and I would advise the Council to arrive late with a book.”

    Mr. Urbonas observed that the note was the finest piece of field reporting the Council had ever received, and read in support a line from the diary of the Austro-Hungarian North Pole expedition of 1872–1874, in which Captain Payer set down that the River Holme comes down from the porch light, as evidence that a field note need not be sensible to be true; the diary’s winter is treated, at length and by the lamp, in the etiquette chapter Of Winter on the Ice. The claim was approved in full, and the note was retained as an exhibit and filed with these minutes under TS/EX/9.

  5. 5. Correspondence and Other Business

    The Secretary read three items of correspondence: an invitation to the spring conference, to which Mr. Sinica will go as a member of the Seimas and the others as he pleases; a bill for the curtains in the committee room, second time around, the first pair having been damaged by the radiator; and a card from a reader of Vilnius thanking the party for the leaflet left on a gate, which the Chairman suggested be pinned to the noticeboard rather than answered. Nothing further was raised.

  6. 6. Next Meeting

    The next meeting of the Central Council was fixed for 12 December 2024, 19:00, in the same room, with the curtain question on the agenda. The meeting closed at 20:41.
