Thursday, 14 October 2004 • Price twenty-five cents • Edition 11-04

Los Angeles, October 2004. The Masquerade holds, the bars close at two, and the haven listings move faster than the blood bank's deliveries. This sheet carries the classifieds of the kindred and the kindred-adjacent: havens for lease in Santa Monica, Hollywood and Chinatown, night goods for sale, services rendered after dark, and the personal columns no daytime paper would print. A fledgling fresh from the Embrace will find clans named — Ventrue, Tremere, Nosferatu, Toreador, Brujah, Gangrel, Malkavian — prices asked, and numbers answered between eight and ten after dark; an older hand will find the catalogue notice under Lost & Found, where a desk clerk has entered the news as received. All advertisements are accepted in good faith; the Masquerade remains the reader's own responsibility.

Real Estate — Havens

Haven — Santa Monica

One-bedroom flat above the blood bank, Ocean Park Boulevard, street level secure with roof access fitted. Blackout shutters already in place; no mirrors in the bathroom. $1,950 per month, paid in advance, cash or kind. References from the seneschal required. Call (310) 555-0147 after dark only.

Townhouse — Chinatown

Four storeys, cellar workshop, warded against scrying. Giovanni Street, quiet end. Share negotiable for a Tremere with laboratory experience. Serious inquiries to Box 814, care of this sheet.

Room to Share — Hollywood

Studio flat above a Vine Street nightclub. Music until two a.m.; bring your own quiet. Suitable for Toreador; unsuitable for Nosferatu. $900 plus half utilities. (213) 555-0192.

For Sale

1974 Cadillac Fleetwood

Black, bulletproof glass, trunk lined in sheet iron. Runs on blood or gasoline, so the owner claims. Recently serviced by a mechanic who never removes his sunglasses. $18,500 or best nocturnal offer. (323) 555-0166.

Blood — O Negative

Fresh, cold, by the pint or the gallon, delivered to haven doors in Hollywood and Santa Monica. Discount for standing orders. Mercurio's, Imperial Freehold. (213) 555-0111.

Wanted

Baker's Third Line

The 12 March order-slip from Dona Luzia's pensão, Pedro do Rosário; specifically the third line, smaller hand, read into the record of the Gazeta da Orquestra. Will pay in farinha d'água, engine grease, or one night's quiet in a haven with blackout shutters. The clerk of the Council of the Single Link stands by his reading: To the Editor — P versus NP and the Third Line of the Order-Slip.

Seat on the Academy Limited

One seat, February or March running, Hollywood Central to the Dolby Grand, any year. Fare unpublished; admission by Academy vote. The passenger declines to say where she alights — though the sheet's own columnists place her last at the Ocean House Hotel, Santa Monica, walking uphill from the terminal. Timetable and platform commentary: The Academy Limited — Timetable of the Academy Awards, February–March.
