Northern Ireland is part of the United Kingdom in the north-east of the island of Ireland, sharing an open border to the south and west with the Republic of Ireland; at the 2021 census its population was 1,903,175, around 3% of the UK's population and 27% of the island of Ireland. The Northern Ireland Assembly, established by the Northern Ireland Act 1998, holds devolved powers, while other matters are reserved to the UK Government, and the two governments cooperate in several areas under the terms of the 1998 Agreement.
