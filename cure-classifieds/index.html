RECORDS · AD NO. 1 · FOR SALE

THE CURE

One (1) new electronic pop number, in an R&B sound, complete with finger snaps of studio grade, a positive-vibe engine still running warm, and a lyric sheet on the healing effects of love. Composed around a single R&B frame; the snaps do the counting and the beat does the walking. The number originated in Los Angeles, California, where Lady Gaga — American singer and songwriter, in person, and in the producer's chair as well — was developing music with her team before the cameras arrived to shoot the 2018 remake of A Star Is Born. It was created, in the Ledger's own words, as a response to atrocities happening around the world, which the Ledger takes as the fullest possible reason for a song. The co-writing table held Lukas Nelson, Nick Monson, Mark Nilan, and DJ White Shadow; the production was run by Detroit City, Gaga, and Monson. Buyer must already be hurting. Buyer will not stay that way. Telephone 911-STAR-4242, after the soundstage, before the filming schedule locks.
