Issue 14,011 · 12 Kč · Thursday, 11 April 2024

The column is pinned above the ticket stubs at Národní třída every Thursday, read on the tram out to Vinohrady, and paid for in koruny at the kiosk by the metro entrance. This week: rooms, wires, washers, Cauliflowers by the head, one red shirt that still remembers Bedminster, and the evidence of a crossbar that never was.

To let

Flat, Vinohrady, Korunní 67 — two rooms and a kitchen looking out over Riegrovy sady. The dining room is low-ceilinged and holds forty-four, with a wire-sifter inside, and a brass washer may be garnished with Cauliflowers. Rent 14,500 Kč per month, heating extra, no dogs, no sublets. Enquire Mrs. Havlíček, between 7 and 9 p.m.

First floor left · Praha 2 · no agents

For sale

Wire-sifter, brass, 1934, from a Kolín mill — still sifts flour for forty-four if you ask it nicely, though nobody has asked in years. 800 Kč. Collect, Brevnov, mornings only.

Brevnov, by the old mill pond · leave a coin under the mat

For sale

Red football shirt, medium, folded away in 2014 and not worn since. The Ashton Gate Road turnstile knew its face. 400 Kč or best offer, serious buyers only.

A. Parry, Bedminster, Bristol · poste restante, Praha hlavní nádraží

Wanted

One brass washer, pocket-sized, last seen at Gate 13, Row H, Seat 14, travelling south toward Cardiff. Finder receives a glass of wine at the next meal, no questions asked.

D. Chen, Clerkenwell · care of the pass, The Coalhouse, 9 Bute Docks, Cardiff

Services

Crossbar evidence given and withdrawn. Eighty-ninth minute affidavits, woodwork rulings, eight to one, the dissenting voice heard in person. Day or night, floodlit or not.

The Matchnight & Trophy Business Committee · via Vinohrady

Lost
